# Books: Russell Brunson

His books, as he teaches them out loud in the 56 podcast transcripts (2017 to 2026). No book text was used; every summary below comes from talks, keynotes and Q&As.

---

### DotCom Secrets (2015; revised edition 2020)
- **Thesis:** A funnel beats a website. Build a value ladder and a break-even front end so you can outspend competitors to acquire customers.
- **Key frameworks:** value ladder, the attractive character, hook story offer, follow-up funnels, front end and back end funnels, whoever spends most to acquire a customer wins.
- **Taught in:** S006, S007, S008, S010, S021, S026, S038, S039, S052.
- **He says:** it was the doctrine for people who already wanted funnels (S038).

### Expert Secrets (2017; revised edition 2020)
- **Thesis:** Experts turn what they know into a movement and sell it one to many with story.
- **Key frameworks:** Epiphany Bridge, the big domino, three core false beliefs, the perfect webinar, new opportunity versus improvement, the stack.
- **Taught in:** S002, S003, S004, S005, S011, S015, S016, S017, S034, S045.
- **He says:** it brought in people who did not yet know they needed a funnel (S038), and his $10,000 offer teaches these same frameworks packaged differently (S051). He tells new coaches to read it first (S052).

### Traffic Secrets (2020)
- **Thesis:** Your dream customers already gather somewhere. Find them, earn and buy your way in, then turn that traffic into a list you own.
- **Key frameworks:** Dream 100, work your way in and buy your way in, earned, controlled and owned traffic, $1 per name per month, hook story offer at the platform level.
- **Taught in:** S014, S022, S023, S024 (he reads chapters live in these 2020 recordings), S051, S052.

### Other books and series (mentioned, not taught at length)
- **108 Proven Split Test Winners:** a book of split test results; not taught in the corpus.
- **The Linchpin:** a free book he promotes on continuity as the core offer that every other product feeds (S052, S053). Its contents appear only in outline.
- **The Secrets trilogy as a set:** a co-host promotes the three books together (S051); no separate teaching.
- **30days.com:** a crowdsourced project built from a summit question; hit a million dollars in about a week and a half (S009).
