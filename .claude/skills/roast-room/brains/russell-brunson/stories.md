# Stories: Russell Brunson

18 stories and case studies he retells, from 56 podcast transcripts (2017 to 2026). One or two lines each, with the lesson he draws.

---

1. **The potato gun DVD.** His first product made money on $10 a day ads until Google raised costs; he went broke until he added a $200 kit upsell that one in three bought. Lesson: funnels make money, websites do not. (S039, S052)
2. **The call center collapse.** A coaching phone-sales company grew to about 100 people, then his merchant accounts were frozen, staff were poached and unpaid payroll taxes left him owing the IRS. A webinar from an empty office kept him alive. Lesson: overhead and growth before cash kill you. (S033, S037, S002)
3. **12 companies in 12 months.** After the collapse he launched small businesses in cold sores, couponing, weight loss and supplements. Customers never crossed over. Lesson: pick one market and stack. (S027, S037, S003)
4. **DotCom Secrets Local.** A $1,000 auto-webinar offer made over a million dollars in 90 days and paid off the IRS debt, sold first to his list, then partners' lists. (S034)
5. **The free CD funnel.** A $4.95 shipping DVD with a sales letter for $5,500 phone coaching and a $37 a month newsletter: 800 buyers, 10 coaching sales, $55,000 in two weeks. Lesson: continuity is the base. (S037)
6. **ClickFunnels' failed launches.** The first five launches failed; a live presentation to about 100 people then sold 35% of the room. Lesson: keep testing; the live pitch is the engine. (S002, S018)
7. **Funnel Hacks webinar.** Done live over 100 times, it sold over $100 million of ClickFunnels. Lesson: one presentation, refined, can carry a company. (S015, S027)
8. **Natalie Hodson.** A fitness mom turned an embarrassing workout video into a $47 pelvic floor ebook with over 120,000 buyers. Lesson: a better hook, not a better product. (S005, S014)
9. **Drew Manning, Fit to Fat to Fit.** A trainer gained weight on purpose; the hook stood out among a thousand ripped-trainer ads. (S005)
10. **Drew Canole and Organifi.** He coached FitLife TV free when it was weeks from closing; Drew's video on a one-page application became his coaching funnel. Lesson: prove it free, then let the story sell. (S032)
11. **Lady Boss.** A weight loss business sold a low-priced lifetime app on a daily webinar, built continuity, then sold $3,000 coaching and supplements behind it. (S049)
12. **Bombed in a stadium.** $3.2 million in 90 minutes at the first 10X event, then a flop in a 35,000-seat stadium the next year. Lesson: message to market match; feel it, then adjust. (S018, S038, S012)
13. **Dean Graziosi and the $100,000 check.** He paid Dean $100,000 on the spot to join first, so he could later charge that himself. Lesson: you cannot charge what you have never paid. (S008)
14. **Rand Fishkin's warning.** The Moz founder told him venture money left him unable to afford a car and pushed into tactics he disliked. ClickFunnels stayed bootstrapped. (S038)
15. **The $110 million each offer.** Around the 2020 election he and Todd turned down a sale to keep building. (S038, S040)
16. **ClickFunnels 2.0.** Announced a year early, too many let in at once, a year of bugs and angry customers. Lesson, in his words, "definitely a Russell mistake". (S038, S040)
17. **The Dan Kennedy mastermind.** He put a $25,000 mastermind on a card he could not afford; the value came from hearing other members' questions. (S031, S052)
18. **Ending Funnel Hacking Live.** Tired, with costs over $6 million and 10 months a year of work, he ended FHL at its peak, then ran a smaller AI encore. Lesson: stop at the peak to make room. (S041, S042)
