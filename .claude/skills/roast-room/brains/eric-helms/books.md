# Books: Eric Helms

Built from how he describes his books in the 34 sources; no book text was fetched. Summaries are in my own words.

## The Muscle and Strength Pyramid: Nutrition (with Andy Morgan and Andrea Valdez; first edition about 2015, second 2019, third edition in progress in 2023 to 2025)
- **Core thesis:** Nutrition has an order of importance. Get the base right and the small details barely matter.
- **Key frameworks:** energy balance first (rate of loss or gain), then macronutrients, micronutrients and water, meal timing and frequency, and supplements last; slow, well-structured diets with refeeds and diet breaks; a post-diet recovery plan. The third edition replaces "good, better, best" tracking options with a calorie range plus minimum protein, fat and carb intakes, and teaches tracking as a skill on the way to habits (S015).
- **Taught in sources:** S001, S002, S015, S016 (the YouTube pyramid series it grew from), S012, S013.

## The Muscle and Strength Pyramid: Training (same co-authors and editions)
- **Core thesis:** Training also has an order: adherence and the basics (volume, intensity, frequency) first, then progression, then exercise selection, then rest periods and tempo.
- **Key frameworks:** hard sets per muscle per week; RIR-based RPE and autoregulation; linear progression of load in intermediate and advanced programs; deloads; antagonist paired sets for time efficiency; novice, intermediate and advanced templates (S006, S009).
- **Taught in sources:** S006, S009, S011, S015.

## Major papers and series that stand in for book material
- **Evidence-based recommendations for natural bodybuilding contest preparation: nutrition and supplementation (2014)** with Alan Aragon and Peter Fitschen; the companion training paper (2015) with Brad Schoenfeld and others (S028, S001).
- **PhD thesis on the RIR-based RPE scale in powerlifting (2017)** (S030), with papers S029, S032, S034.
- **Towards a Sustainable Nutrition Paradigm in Physique Sport (2019)** (S031): mental health as the needed shift in bodybuilding nutrition.
- **MASS Research Review (since 2017)**, monthly with Greg Nuckols, Mike Zourdos, later Eric Trexler and Lauren Colenso-Semple; cover stories S022 to S027, S033.
