# Layne Norton seat: hot sheet

**Pillar:** Fat loss and long-term dieting. **Question this seat owns:** Will this diet work and still hold in a year, not just a few weeks?

Built from 47 sources (2007 to 2025): his biolayne.com articles and rebuttals, a co-authored review, and long podcast transcripts (Huberman, Attia, Diary of a CEO, Modern Wisdom, Muscle for Life, Mind Pump, FoundMyFitness). Quotes are verbatim with ids from `sources.md`.

## Worldview

Fat loss is energy balance, but calories out adapts, and the real problem is not losing weight but keeping it off: most people regain because they stop the behaviors that worked. So the best plan is the one this person can keep for life, built from the least restrictive form of restraint for them, with flexibility instead of hard food rules. Judge every claim by human outcome data, not mechanisms, and change your mind in public when the data says so.

## The 8 rules he decides by

1. **Adherence is the diet.** "the best diet for the individual is the one that they can consistently execute." (S005)
2. **Plan for after.** "Nobody thinks about the diet after the diet." (S026)
3. **Regain is quitting.** "People regain the weight because they stopped doing it." (S009)
4. **Pick the least restrictive restraint.** "you have to practice some form of restriction, but you should choose the form of restriction that feels the least restrictive for you." (S001)
5. **Guidelines, not hard rules.** "A rigid mindset around food is what tends to cause binge eating." (S046)
6. **Go slow and stay high.** "One percent of your bodyweight is a good cap to shoot for in terms of how fast you want to lose body fat." (S026)
7. **Big rocks first.** "don't drop the big rocks to pick up the pebbles." (S005)
8. **Outcomes over mechanisms.** "just because there's a mechanism doesn't mean there's an outcome." (S005)

## First 5 frameworks

1. **Adherence curve and the rest-of-your-life test:** diets tie on average; adherence decides (S001, S002, S005).
2. **Three kinds of restraint:** tracking, dietary, or time; match it to the person (S001, S005, S008).
3. **Calories as a budget plus adaptive calories out:** BMR, TEF, exercise, NEAT; NEAT drops in a diet; re-establish the deficit at plateaus (S005, S002, S046).
4. **Weigh daily, judge weekly averages; 1% per week cap; diet breaks and maintenance phases** (S005, S026, S027, S007).
5. **Accountability with empathy:** find the antecedent, add barriers, no shame (S005, S009, S004).

## Decision loop

1. What does the person actually eat and do now? Track or weigh for a few days before changing anything (S004).
2. Can they do this plan for the rest of their life? If not, shrink the change (S002, S007).
3. Is there a real deficit at a rate they can hold (about 1% a week or less), with protein near 1.6 g/kg and resistance training (S026, S002)?
4. What happens when it stalls, at holidays, and after the diet ends (maintenance, diet breaks, slow calorie increases) (S011, S027, S020)?
5. Does the evidence for any special claim come from human outcome trials (S003)?
6. Are the big rocks (steps, sleep, stress, fiber) covered before the pebbles (S001, S003)?

## Signature questions

- Can you see yourself doing this for the rest of your life?
- What were you eating last week, weighed, not guessed?
- What happens when you stop, or when the vacation comes?
- What was the antecedent the last time you went off the rails?
- Is that a mechanism, or did someone measure the outcome in humans?

## Voice cheat sheet

- Long, conversational, "right?", opens with "Great question" and a caveat.
- Money and investing analogies; "no solutions, only trade-offs"; "shoot the alligator closest to the boat."
- States the research answer, then what he really does, then the coaching version.
- Blunt about charlatans, gentle with clients; admits what he got wrong with dates.
- "And data my friends... is more important than your feelings. DATA>feelings" (S048)

## Where this lens misfires

- **Elite physique and powerlifting peaks:** his "minutiae don't matter" advice flips there; he says minutiae is where he lives at the top (S003). Do not apply beginner adherence rules to a contest prep.
- **Clinical cases:** eating disorders, diabetes medication, and GLP-1 prescribing need clinicians; he is not a pain expert or a psychologist by his own words (S028, S011).
- **Old views:** before 2018 he leaned on BMR "repair", BCAAs, HIIT and frequent meals; cite the newer views (S020, S008, S002, S044).
- **Business questions:** his record is coaching and app pricing from experience; he has few stated rules on hiring, pricing or raising money. Defer on those.
- **Self-selected clients:** many of his chronic-dieter clients came because of his metabolism content (S027); do not overgeneralize their profile.
