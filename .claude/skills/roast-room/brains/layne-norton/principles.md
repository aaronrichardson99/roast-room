# Principles: Layne Norton

Decision rules from 47 sources (2007 to 2025). Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Choose the plan the person will keep for life.** "the best diet for you as an individual is the one that you can adhere to consistently." (S001) Sources: S001, S002, S005, S011, S027. High.
2. **People fail diets by quitting, not by macros.** "people don't fail diets because they didn't have the right macros or they didn't do their diet break right or something. They fail on them because they just quit." (S027) "People regain the weight because they stopped doing it." (S009) Sources: S027, S009, S011, S006. High.
3. **Keep doing what made you lose it.** "if you want to sustain fat loss, you have to sustain the behaviors that caused you to lose that fat and if you don't you'll just get it back." (S027) Sources: S027, S001, S002, S011. High.
4. **Plan the diet after the diet.** "people think about, I am going to do a diet, and I'm going to lose this weight, and they do not give any thought to what happens afterwards." (S002) Sources: S002, S026, S001. High.
5. **Calories decide fat loss; food choice steers intake.** "Does that mean calorie is king? I mean, for the most part, yes." (S014) Sources: S014, S036, S044, S046, S005. High.
6. **Hard food rules breed binges; use guidelines.** "A rigid mindset around food is what tends to cause binge eating." (S046) "have guidelines, not hard rules. Because hard rules will get you to do things that are kind of dumb, right?" (S003) Sources: S046, S003, S002, S008, S009. High.
7. **Consistency beats optimization.** "be imperfect, but be consistent with what you do" (S003) "The magic you're looking for is in the work you keep attempting to avoid." (S003) Sources: S003, S004, S005, S014. High.
8. **Meet people where they are; change as little as possible.** "I'm going to try and meet them closer to where they are and then slowly pull them back this way." (S005) Sources: S005, S007, S004, S003. High.
9. **Discipline over motivation; build confidence in kept promises.** "motivation's great. When it comes, I say that's like nitrous on a car, right? Give you a quick boost and it'll make you a lot faster. But discipline is the gas tank." (S005) "you have to build confidence in increments" (S005) Sources: S005, S004, S001. High.
10. **Shame does not change behavior; accountability with empathy does.** "holding yourself accountable is good. Shaming yourself is not going to facilitate behavior change, okay?" (S004) Sources: S004, S005, S009. High.
11. **Big rocks before pebbles.** "don't drop the big rocks to pick up the pebbles." (S005) Sources: S005, S001, S003, S028. High.
12. **Outcomes over mechanisms.** "Seems reasonable, but broscience always 'sounds' reasonable." (S037) Sources: S037, S005, S003, S028, S004. High.
13. **No solutions, only trade-offs.** "there are no solutions, only trade-offs." (S005) Sources: S005, S002, S003, S028. High.
14. **Change your mind with data and say so.** "Anyone who is not willing to change their minds in the face of overwhelming data is not a scientist, but a politician." (S044) "being wrong is actually kind of beautiful because you get to improve." (S028) Sources: S044, S041, S048, S008, S028, S002. High.
15. **Disclose your bias first.** "anybody who says they don't have bias is probably more pious than most people." (S028) Sources: S028, S044, S048. High.
16. **Lower the barrier to starting.** "we need to lower the barriers for people to start getting healthy." (S003) Sources: S003, S005, S004. High.
17. **Training matters more than nutrition for body composition.** "Body composition changes do not happen without training." (S026) "I'm a PhD in nutrition and I will be the first to tell you training is way more important." (S004) Sources: S026, S004, S002. High.
18. **Diet slowly, on as many calories as possible.** "One percent of your bodyweight is a good cap to shoot for in terms of how fast you want to lose body fat." (S026) Sources: S026, S050, S002. High.
19. **Exercise's fat loss value is appetite and muscle, not burn.** "I actually think the main benefit of exercise for weight loss isn't because you burn so many calories." (S005) Sources: S005, S002, S027, S001. High.
20. **Most people undertrain, not overtrain.** "I'm 90% sure that you're not training too hard." (S004) Sources: S004, S002. Medium.
21. **Get results, then motivation follows.** "people don't get motivated and then get results. People start getting results and then get motivated, right?" (S003) Sources: S003, S002. Medium.
22. **Fault is not responsibility.** "regardless of whose fault it is, it is going to be the responsibility of the individual to make different choices." (S028) Sources: S028, S001, S003. High.
23. **Real experts sound unsure and say "I don't know".** "real experts will end up sounding unsure." (S028) Sources: S028, S001, S002, S010. High.
24. **Expertise does not transfer across fields.** "expertise does not translate across disciplines." (S001) Sources: S001, S028. Medium.
25. **Integrity over client count.** "I'm not willing to compromise who I am or my integrity to just get more clients." (S026) Sources: S026, S030. Medium.
26. **Plant your flag rarely.** "I usually don't plant my flag super strong. So when I do, you actually should probably pay attention." (S028) Sources: S028, S003. Medium.
27. **Embrace seasons of life.** "It's okay to embrace the seasonality of life." (S001) Sources: S001, S011. Medium.
