# Books: Layne Norton

Built from how he describes his books in interviews (S027, S044, S046, S026). No book text is reproduced here; the corpus holds no book chapters.

## Fat Loss Forever (2019, ebook)
- **Thesis:** Most diets work short term and fail long term; the book spends as much time on keeping fat off as losing it. He calls it his fat loss manifesto, nearly 400 pages, half guide and half theory (S027).
- **Key frameworks taught:** energy balance with adaptive calories out; the fat balance figure (fat stored minus fat burned, so low carb and low fat net out the same) (S044, S046); why diets fail and the traits of people who keep weight off; how to periodize a long diet for people with a lot to lose; the post-diet phase (S027).
- **Taught in:** S027, S044, S046, S005.

## The Complete Contest Prep Guide (ebook)
- **Thesis:** A step by step manual to coach yourself for a bodybuilding, physique or bikini show, written like a textbook (S027).
- **Key frameworks:** slow prep with enough time to get lean; minimal peak week changes, no water or sodium cuts (S026, S030, S034).
- **Taught in:** S027, S026.

## The Complete Reverse Dieting Guide (ebook with Sohee Lee)
- **Thesis:** After a diet, add calories slowly to limit fat regain and raise the intake you maintain on (S026). His later view (S020) is that the effect works mostly through activity, not BMR.
- **Taught in:** S026, S024, S020.
