# Principles: Morgan Housel

26 decision rules, built from his essays (2017 to 2026) and long interviews (2022 to 2026). "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Never take a risk that can wipe you out, whatever the odds.** "no risk that could wipe you out is ever worth taking." (S011) "If you risk what you need in order to gain what you don't need, that is foolish." (Buffett line he quotes, S034) Sources: 6 (S001, S011, S016, S017, S034, S044). Confidence: high.

2. **Keep a buffer that feels like too much.** "you have to have a level of savings in your asset allocation that doesn't make sense. You have to have a level of conservatism that seems like it's a little bit too much." (S001) "The amount of cash you should have should feel like it's too much." (S004) Sources: 5 (S001, S004, S017, S027, S055). Confidence: high.

3. **Use money, and choices, to buy control over your time first.** "what people really want in life is independence and autonomy." (S001) "Money's greatest intrinsic value is its ability to give you control over your time." (S044) Sources: 9 (S001, S004, S005, S010, S012, S039, S044, S055, S059). Confidence: high.

4. **Want less; it is the lever you control most.** "it's more in your control to just inside your head to say, I'm going to try to want less." (S004) "Sacrifice more or want less. Those are your two options." (S005) Sources: 6 (S004, S005, S019, S039, S053, S063). Confidence: high.

5. **Keep expectations rising slower than results.** "If expectations grow faster than income you'll never be happy with your money." (S019) "Your expectations are a debt that has to be repaid." (S005) Sources: 8 (S004, S005, S007, S013, S019, S020, S044, S063). Confidence: high.

6. **Stop trying to impress strangers; pick the few whose love you want.** "Independence does not mean you don't care what anyone thinks of you. It means that you strategically decide whose attention you seek." (S022) Sources: 7 (S002, S004, S005, S010, S022, S063, S065). Confidence: high.

7. **Pick what you can keep doing for decades over what is best this year.** "It's about earning pretty good returns that you can stick with for a long period of time." (S011) "If I can't keep it going, I'm not interested in it." (S002) Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S004, S008, S011, S049). Confidence: high.

8. **Do not push for 100 percent; it ends in burnout.** "If you're always trying to squeeze out 100 percent potential for something, almost certainly it's going to lead to burnout" (S002) Sources: 2 (S002, S007). Confidence: medium.

9. **Never tie your identity to something you cannot sustain.** "If you attach your identity to something that you cannot sustain when it ends, you're going to be morally crushed." (S002) "Be careful what beliefs you let become part of your identity" (S058) Sources: 3 (S002, S024, S058). Confidence: high.

10. **Copy only what is repeatable from the people you admire.** "you can't emulate their luck. You probably can't emulate their super special talent or intellect. But there are things among them that you can emulate." (S001) "It is wrong to idolize the life that they live." (S010) Sources: 5 (S001, S005, S009, S010, S037). Confidence: high.

11. **You cannot take the success and skip its costs.** "if you want the success, but you want to eliminate the downsides of it, you're completely fooling yourself. You have to make a decision." (S002) "you can't pick bits and pieces. You have to take the whole thing." (S005) Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S005, S010, S039). Confidence: high.

12. **Treat setbacks as the fee, not a fine, and pay it.** "It's the fee and not the penalty." (S002) "Everything worthwhile has a price, and most of those prices are hidden." (S039) Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S004, S011, S030, S039). Confidence: high.

13. **Be reasonable, not coldly rational.** "Rational is hard. Being reasonable is often the most practical goal." (S027) "You can pretend you're rational, but usually it's just a self justification for the irrational things you're doing." (S010) Sources: 4 (S001, S005, S010, S027). Confidence: high.

14. **Plan for your panicked self, not your calm self.** "The difference between what you think and what you actually do can be 10 miles apart" (S001) "most people realize that their risk tolerance is actually much less than they thought." (S008) Sources: 4 (S001, S008, S009, S010). Confidence: high.

15. **Plan on the plan not going to plan.** "the most important part of every plan is to plan on the plan not going according to plan." (S017) "Plan on things not going according to plan." (S057) Sources: 3 (S008, S017, S057). Confidence: high.

16. **Judge less: other people are often playing a different game.** "Judge less." (S031) "A lot of financial mistakes come from decisions that would be right for someone else but wrong for you." (S022) Sources: 6 (S005, S011, S022, S031, S046, S063). Confidence: high.

17. **Credit luck, in both directions, including in yourself.** "not all success is due to hard work, and not all poverty is due to laziness. Keep this in mind when judging people, including yourself." (S011) "the luckier you are, the nicer you should be." (S036) Sources: 5 (S002, S011, S036, S039, S044). Confidence: high.

18. **When success makes you sure, get humbler.** "Getting rich can be the biggest impediment to staying rich." (S016) "their success increases their confidence more than their ability." (S004) Sources: 5 (S004, S008, S016, S036, S037). Confidence: high.

19. **Keep some real challenge in your life; boredom is harder than work.** "everybody needs a minimum level of stress in their life." (S004) "if you think work is hard, try boredom. It's way harder." (S007) Sources: 4 (S004, S007, S021, S047). Confidence: high.

20. **Turn far goals into short systems and do not over-optimize them.** "You're taking what is effectively a long-term goal. and you're turning it into a short-term system." (S007) "It's not detailed, but it's good enough." (S018) Sources: 3 (S007, S018, S057). Confidence: high.

21. **Protect unstructured time to think.** "taking time in the middle of your day to do stuff that doesn't look like work is the most important part of your work day." (S032) Sources: 4 (S001, S008, S032, S033). Confidence: high.

22. **Avoid the assured miseries before chasing joy.** "It's often hard to know what will bring joy but easy to spot what will bring misery." (S045) "So you can move the needle a lot by focusing on what not to do in life." (S063) Sources: 3 (S045, S060, S063). Confidence: high.

23. **Remove FOMO.** "FOMO is recklessness masked as ambition." (S060) "Having no FOMO might be the most important investing skill." (S044) Sources: 3 (S041, S044, S060). Confidence: high.

24. **Change your mind when the facts change.** "It's the ability to quickly abandon previous beliefs when the world changes or when you come across new information." (S058) "Forgiving yourself for changing your mind is a superpower, especially when you're young." (S039) Sources: 4 (S009, S039, S058, S066). Confidence: high.

25. **Do not rush; forcing speed is where mistakes come from.** "Most financial mistakes come when you try to force things to happen faster than is required." (S044) "however fast something grows, that's the half-life in which it can be destroyed." (S008) Sources: 4 (S008, S037, S044, S053). Confidence: high.

26. **Ask what you will regret, not what is safest.** "you want to have as few regrets as possible, not take as few risks as you can" (S005) "Maybe regret is the best definition of risk." (S034) Sources: 2 (S005, S034). Confidence: medium.
