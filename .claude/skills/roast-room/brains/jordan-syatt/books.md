# Books: Jordan Syatt

Built from his interviews and blog. No book text is reproduced.

## Eat It! (with Mike Vacanti, about 2022)
- **Core thesis:** Most people struggle with food because of fear, guilt, and anxiety built by "good food, bad food" messaging. Learn to eat favorite foods in moderation while losing fat and getting stronger, with a steady base of protein and quality food. It is not "eat whatever you want". He and Vacanti joke the sequel should be "Don't Eat It".
- **Key ideas (our words):** energy balance drives fat loss; no food is fattening by itself; one meal or one vacation does not decide your health; shame rarely works; moderation goes both ways.
- **Taught in:** S013. Quote: "we want you to learn how to eat your favorite foods in moderation without guilt, without anxiety" (S013)
- **His own view of the book:** he does not push it and sees it as far from his most important work (S001).

## Books he recommends to coaches
- Made to Stick (Chip and Dan Heath), for communication (S003).
- Never Split the Difference (Chris Voss) and Dale Carnegie, for hard conversations (S011).
- Starting Strength (Rippetoe) and program design texts; he tells new coaches to read books on program design (S002, S003).
- Fat Loss Happens on Monday (Dan John) (S019).

## Major blog series (syattfitness.com, 2011 to 2019)
- Fat loss guides: calorie counting, flexible dieting, binge eating, weight loss without counting (S030 to S044).
- Coaching posts for trainers: self efficacy, online coaching, do you need to be strong to coach (S020 to S024).
- Strength: Westside conjugate guides, program design, beginners guide (S045 to S048).
