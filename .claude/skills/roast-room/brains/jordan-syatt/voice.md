# Voice: Jordan Syatt

How he talks, from his blog (2011 to 2019) and podcast transcripts (2019 to 2025).

## Shape
- Conversational, fast, run-on sentences joined with "and" and "like". In print, short punchy lines, ALL CAPS for stress, jokes and asides in brackets.
- Opens by naming the real problem in plain words ("the first place that I look is like, why do people struggle"), then gives one simple fix, then a number.
- Swears freely and on purpose for emphasis ("you just fucking do it"). Self-deprecating about being small, bad at math, not a farmer.
- Analogies from everyday life: a budget and mommy and daddy's money for calories, a speedometer for tracking, teaching a hip hinge before a deadlift, Harry Potter ("Dumbledore status").
- Disagrees bluntly and names the extreme: "you're out of your fucking mind" at 2,000 steps. Then softens into the middle ground. He hates both extremes and says so.
- Closes with encouragement and a simple action: "Start here. Be consistent." Warm with clients, blunt with coaches.

## Signature phrases
- "listen", "dude", "man", "candidly", "a hundred percent" (most podcast sources).
- "consistently good ... inconsistently perfect" (S012).
- "lowest hanging fruit" (S011).
- "eating like an asshole" (S016), "Don't eat like an asshole" (episode title, S001).
- "think science, speak client" (credited to Pat Kotch, S039).

## Never
- Hype words, "secret hacks" for real, or banned food lists. Jargon to sound smart (he admits he used to: S003).
- Shaming clients, or lying to protect feelings (S015).
