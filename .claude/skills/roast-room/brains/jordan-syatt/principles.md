# Principles: Jordan Syatt

Decision rules from 47 public sources (2011 to 2025). Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Help people first; money follows.** "if your main goal is to make money, I don't wanna work with you." (S001) "I think the more you help people, the more money you're probably going to make." (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S003, S007). High.
2. **Consistency beats perfection.** "consistently good is infinitely better than inconsistently. Perfect." (S012) Sources: 4 (S012, S019, S040, S013). High.
3. **Coach in person before online.** "I think if you haven't coach people in person at all, then you can't be the best coach." (S002) Sources: 3 (S001, S002, S003). High.
4. **Build on word of mouth.** "I think that a word of mouth business is the best business you can build." (S003) Sources: 3 (S001, S002, S003). High.
5. **No good or bad foods.** "I adamantly, firmly and severely do not believe in good foods and bad foods." (S040) Sources: 4 (S040, S016, S013, S039). High.
6. **Be honest, even when it costs a sale.** "I'm not gonna lie to them and be like, yeah, you'll never be hungry in a calorie deficit." (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S015, S016). High.
7. **Speak client, not coach.** "Most clients don’t give a shit about the science behind nutrition. They just want to look good naked." (S039) "I was using a lot of bigger terminology, not because it was necessary for the article, but because I wanted to sound smart." (S003) Sources: 2. Medium.
8. **Ignore what other coaches think.** "worrying about what other coaches are going to say is just it's a terrible rabbit hole to go down because number one, they're not going to be the ones who are going to be paying you." (S003) Sources: 1. Low.
9. **Study books; feeds are not education.** "Instagram TikTok doesn't count as education" (S002) Sources: 2 (S002, S003). Medium.
10. **Knowledge comes before charm.** "a great coach starts with knowledge, then from there being a great listener and also being able to communicate" (S002) Sources: 1. Low.
11. **Expect slow, nonlinear progress.** "Stop expecting progress to be linear." (S019) Sources: 3 (S019, S020, S001). High.
12. **Pick one program and stay on it.** "Pick ONE program and follow it perfectly for at least 3 months." (S019) Sources: 1. Low.
13. **Train 3 or 4 days and leave a rep in the tank.** "98% of my clients — myself included — train 3 or 4 times/week." (S019) "My best strength gains have always come from lifting in the 80 – 90% 1RM range, making sure to leave one solid rep in the tank at the end of every set." (S019) Sources: 1. Low.
14. **Small wins build belief.** "starting small and building momentum with consistent success will drastically improve self efficacy and facilitate long-term adherence." (S024) Sources: 2 (S024, S011). Medium.
15. **Practice what you preach.** "if they don’t have practical experience with what their clients are going through…they’re going to be at a massive disadvantage." (S020) Sources: 2 (S020, S039). Medium.
16. **One meal never decides anything.** "your health is not dictated by a single food, by a single meal, by a single day." (S013) Sources: 2 (S013, S040). Medium.
17. **The only failure is quitting.** "The only way you will screw up, is if you GIVE UP." (S040) Sources: 2 (S040, S012). Medium.
18. **Refer out what is not yours.** "there are people who are specialists who can work with you" (S040) Sources: 1. Low.
19. **Food first, supplements second.** "If you're taking the supplement and then your plate doesn't look any different. That's a fucking problem" (S012) Sources: 2 (S012, S016). Medium.
20. **Humor sells better than copy.** "I think that if you can just make someone laugh, the sales copy just writes itself at that point." (S012) Sources: 1. Low.
21. **Extremes get attention and lose trust.** "the quickest way to get attention is to say something very extreme." (S015) "It dilutes your brand, it dilutes people from trusting you." (S015) Sources: 2 (S015, S012). Medium.
22. **Fear is not a reason to turn away a client.** "if they don't work with you, they're gonna work with someone. And do you feel like you can provide a safer program for them than someone else? If the answer is yes, then take them on." (S002) Sources: 1. Low.
