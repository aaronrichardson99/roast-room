# Positions: Jordan Syatt

His stances on fitness coaching questions, from 47 public sources (2011 to 2025). Dates are source dates. Where his view changed, both are shown and the newer is marked.

## Running a coaching business

- **Why become a coach:** to help people, not to get rich. "if your main goal is to make money, I don't wanna work with you." (S001, 2025)
- **Get-rich coaching gurus and masterminds:** against. "anytime there's a place for someone to make money, like the industry around selling coaches on how to get rich quick, you're getting a lot of horse shit." (S001, 2025) "A lot of the masterminds are focused on make money as quickly as possible" (S005, 2022)
- **First years:** expect little money. "the first year or two, you're not going to make much money if at all." (S001, 2025)
- **Online only from day one:** against. "anytime someone tells me they just want to start off as an online coach because it's going to make more money ... it's just like, that's stupid." (S001, 2025)
- **Online versus in person (changed):** 2015: "I’d even go so far as to say online personal training is MORE effective than in-person." (S020) Newer (2019 to 2025): coach in person first; it makes a better online coach (S003, S002, S001).
- **Getting clients:** in person, results, referrals; then online. "The best way to build a business is especially fitness business, in person, becoming a great coach, helping people getting referrals." (S001, 2025)
- **First price:** free for a few weeks, then $20, then $40 for 12 weeks, raised with confidence (S002, 2023).
- **Pricing now:** low cost at scale. Inner Circle $24.99 a month billed quarterly; coaches mentorship $1,500 then $99 a month (S005, 2022). "I want to continue to help people at a very, very, very affordable cost" (S007, 2024)
- **Billing terms:** quarterly, so members stay long enough to see results. "if someone buys something for one month at a time, they often expect results immediately." (S005, 2022)
- **One-on-one capacity:** limited spots (seven applicants in 2015, S020); quality drops past a point (S003, 2019).
- **Ads and sponsors:** none on his feed in 2022. "I never do paid content." (S005) "I don't have advertisements on my podcast." (S005) Treat this as a 2022 snapshot; the corpus has no later statement on ads.
- **Free content:** give it all away; clients pay for you. "They're paying for coaches. They're paying for you." (S003, 2019)
- **Social media posting volume:** volume plus consistency built his audience (three posts a day for six months under Gary's push, 5,000 to 200,000 followers). "no one will get tired of good content." (S005, 2022) Newer (2023 to 2024): tired of social media; wants to rely on his community and post less (S007, S011).
- **Content style:** humor and personality over sales copy. "I found humor is the single best way to break down that wall." (S012, 2023)
- **Viral, inflammatory content:** against. "now people are, Specifically making inflammatory content because they know it's going to go viral." (S012, 2023)
- **Personal brand:** an advantage for a solo coach (S012, 2023).
- **Coaching skill versus marketing:** coaching skill grows the business. "the more you make them better coaches, the better their business grows." (S003, 2019)
- **Retention:** word of mouth and past buyers. "the person who is most likely to buy from you ... is someone who's already bought from you." (S002, 2023)
- **Imposter syndrome:** normal; take the client if you are safer than the alternative (S002, 2023).
- **Growth:** no empire. "I don't want an empire to be very blunt." (S007, 2024)
- **GLP-1s and coaches (2025):** an opportunity, not a threat. "I think the odds of someone going to a GLP-1 and wanting a coach is higher than someone not." (S001) "it's gonna be pre and post GLP-1." (S001)
- **Client mix:** "most of my clients are between like 35 to 65. Yeah, and mostly women." (S005, 2022)

## Training

- **Frequency:** "98% of my clients — myself included — train 3 or 4 times/week." (S019, 2016)
- **Intensity and failure:** leave a rep in the tank; 80 to 90 percent for strength (S019, 2016). For power work, never grind. "If you start failing, if you start grinding, you are training the wrong system." (S012, 2023)
- **Program hopping:** against. "Pick ONE program and follow it perfectly for at least 3 months." (S019, 2016)
- **Soreness:** not a goal. "There is legitimately no scientific evidence showing soreness leads to better results." (S019, 2016)
- **Volume of effort for normal people:** less than people think, but not nothing. "I very much believe people don't need to do as much as they've historically been told they need to do." (S012, 2023)
- **Steps:** 7,500 to 10,000; 2,000 is "dangerously low" (S012, 2023).
- **Cardio (changed):** as a young powerlifter he heard cardio downplayed (S011). Newer (2023 to 2024): zone two for most conditioning, some high intensity for intermediates (S012).
- **Mobility (2024):** now a priority; "It takes about six times longer to gain flexibility than it does to maintain it." (S011)
- **Beginners:** strength training plus walking first; build belief with small PRs (S024, S012).

## Nutrition

- **Fat loss:** a calorie deficit drives it. "The main driver of fat loss is total calories in vs. calories out." (S019, 2016)
- **Calorie counting:** do it briefly to learn, then eat by eye. "if you've never tracked your calories and macros, you just fucking do it Like for a week." (S012, 2023) "You don't need to know every single calorie." (S007, 2024)
- **Banned foods:** none. "I adamantly, firmly and severely do not believe in good foods and bad foods." (S040, 2019)
- **Flexible dieting:** a skill some clients are not ready for. "Not everyone is ready for flexible dieting." (S039, 2015)
- **Protein:** a priority; no absorption limit myth. "There’s no limit to how much protein your body can absorb" (S019, 2016)
- **Protein and fiber:** "if you focus on protein and fiber at every meal, it's very hard to overeat." (S012, 2023)
- **Supplements:** food first; creatine is well researched; dislikes protein bars (S012, S019, S016).
- **Fasting:** a tool, but long fasts around binges are a red flag. "you do realize, like, that's not normal." (S007, 2024)
- **Alcohol:** the problem is eating and missed training after it (S016, 2019).
- **Body fat and health:** most people should lose fat. "less fat, more muscle. Is going to improve not only your longevity, like how long you live, but also your quality of your life. End of story." (S011, 2024) Below about 18 percent for men, weigh it against family and mental health (S011).
- **Body positivity:** original intent good; "healthy at every size" wrong; no shaming, no lying (S015, 2021).
- **Binge eating:** not a food issue; see a specialist; 20-minute bright line (S040, 2019).

## Evidence and myths
- **Fitness myths:** the industry swings between extremes. "The fitness industry runs on a pendulum of extremes" (S015, 2021)
- **Evidence versus anecdote:** uses research, but leans on in-person experience and client results; cites studies loosely (inferred from S011, S012).
