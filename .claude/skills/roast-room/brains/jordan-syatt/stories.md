# Stories: Jordan Syatt

The stories he reuses, from 47 public sources (2011 to 2025).

1. **How he got Gary Vaynerchuk.** Mike Vacanti commented on his articles in 2012, later became Gary's coach, and recommended him. Lesson: years of free, consistent content build the network that pays off. (S003)
2. **No idea it was a business.** He started writing to learn, got about 20 views a day, and coached for free. "I didn't know PayPal exists, I was like, I'll do it for free." (S003) Lesson: enjoy the work before you expect money.
3. **Three posts a day.** Gary told him to post three times a day; he posted twice, lied, and got caught. Then he did it every day for six months and went from about 5,000 to over 200,000 followers. Lesson: volume and consistency. (S005)
4. **The Big Mac diet.** One Big Mac a day for 30 days at about 1,500 calories; lost about seven pounds. "I ate one Big Mac a day every day for 30 days straight." (S016) Lesson: energy balance, and freedom from food guilt.
5. **The lasagna photos.** A client swore their calories were in range; 72 hours of meal photos showed a tray of lasagna. Lesson: check portions before chasing details. (S007)
6. **The drinking client.** Three months, no change, 12 to 14 drinks a week. Lesson: an honest talk about priorities beats a new plan. (S016)
7. **The cousin's son.** A 16-year-old wanted to be an online coach "to make a lot of money" after watching a guru. He told him to train people in person first. (S001)
8. **The 22-year-old bored of writing programs.** She had read no books on program design. Lesson: study first. (S002)
9. **Kelly and the scale.** A client in her 40s skipped the scale to avoid anxiety; they tracked measurements, photos, and clothes. Lesson: choose the metric the client can live with. (S020)
10. **Rich kept it off.** Lost 89 pounds and kept it off for 18 months with a "simple, sensible, sustainable approach." Lesson: maintenance is the real result. (S020)
11. **Edith and the hip hinge.** You would not teach a deconditioned client a conventional deadlift first; do not teach flexible dieting first either. (S039)
12. **Weighing peanut butter.** "If you wanna see someone cry, have them weigh out a true measurement of peanut butter for the first time in their life." (S012) Lesson: a short spell of tracking teaches portions.
13. **Friends who died fit.** Strong friends and colleagues died or had strokes after skipping cardio, blood work, and sleep. It changed his view of health. (S011)
14. **His own body fat.** He stays around 15 to 16 percent; a history of disordered eating from wrestling makes going lower not worth it. (S011)
15. **Coaching coaches with Mike Vacanti.** Tired of guru ads, they built an affordable mentorship about coaching skill, not ads. (S002, S005)
