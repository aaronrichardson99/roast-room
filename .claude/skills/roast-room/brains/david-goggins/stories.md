# Stories: David Goggins

Built from 22 sources (2017 to 2026). 18 stories he reuses, with the lesson he draws. Source ids in parentheses. These are his own accounts; some details (weights, counts, dates) vary between tellings.

1. **The abusive father in Buffalo.** He worked his father's skating rink and bar as a small child, rarely went to school, and watched his father beat his mother; at about eight he jumped on his father's back as his father dragged her down the stairs. They fled to Brazil, Indiana. Lesson: the origin of the fear, and later of the habit of studying people (S001, S007, S010, S006).
2. **Racism in Brazil, Indiana, and the principal.** "Nigger, we're gonna kill you" on his notebook and car, a noose in his locker; the principal only said they spelled it wrong. Lesson: nobody is coming to help, which felt like relief (S001, S013, S007).
3. **The accountability mirror at 16 and 17.** A letter said he would not graduate; he had a fourth-grade reading level and had cheated through school. He wrote his faults on sticky notes on the mirror. Lesson: it was him, not anyone else (S005, S007, S018, S020).
4. **The ASVAB and the spiral notebooks.** He failed the test, got a tutor one hour a week, and filled notebooks writing the same thing over and over until he passed. Lesson: his work ethic was invented at the kitchen table, not on runs (S011, S001, S007, S026).
5. **Quitting pararescue.** Afraid of the water, he used his sickle cell trait as cover and quit Air Force pararescue training; he became a TACP and gained weight. Lesson: the cost of quitting and the "only once" trap (S001, S007, S021).
6. **297 pounds, cockroaches, and class 224.** Spraying for cockroaches at night, eating mini donuts and milkshakes, he saw a Discovery show on SEAL Hell Week, quit his job in a cockroach-filled restaurant, and lost 106 pounds in under three months. Lesson: the decision point (S001, S002, S007, S014, S022).
7. **The first quarter-mile run.** He planned four miles, ran a quarter mile, walked home and cried, then went back out. Lesson: failure is part of the process (S001, S024).
8. **Three Hell Weeks in one year.** Rolled back for pneumonia, a broken kneecap, and stress fractures; duct-taped his legs at 3:30 a.m. in the dive cage; a classmate died. Lesson: the mind can drag the body, and make the hard thing the new norm. He says not to copy it (S001, S011, S019, S026).
9. **Taking the instructors' souls.** On the Wednesday of Hell Week he used effort to break the instructors' egos. Lesson: find the fragile ego, starting with your own (S006).
10. **The first 100-mile race.** With no training, Ritz crackers and a lawn chair, he broke down at mile 70, wet himself and bled, then ran 101 miles in about 19 hours, and refused painkillers in the tub. Lesson: the source of the 40% rule and "quit, but not yet" (S001, S007, S009, S019, S024).
11. **Badwater and the Special Operations Warrior Foundation.** He ran to raise money for the families of fallen special operators; at Badwater he walked much of the race and finished fifth, then third. Lesson: readjust the goal, not the effort (S001, S005, S009).
12. **The pull-up record.** Two public failures, 67,000 training pull-ups, then 4,030 in 17 hours; he felt relief, not joy. Lesson: he does it because he hates it (S001, S019).
13. **The body breaks.** Around 38 his body shut down (tight psoas, adrenal and thyroid problems, two heart surgeries for a hole in his heart). Years of stretching restored him, and the forced stillness let him reflect and find peace. Lesson: he learned the hard way; reflect (S001, S010, S011, S021).
14. **The MIT panel.** An academic said people are born a certain way; Goggins contradicted him from lived experience. Lesson: practitioner over theorist (S001).
15. **Going back to see his father.** He went back to Buffalo to understand his father, found his father had been beaten in front of a furnace, and stopped looking for an apology. Lesson: the live autopsy and letting go of hate (S002, S007, S009, S013).
16. **Moab 240 and the telephone pole.** Lost and sick, pulled from the race, he woke at 3 a.m. and went back to finish the miles unofficially by a telephone pole. Lesson: do it because you can, not for glory (S009, S023).
17. **Smokejumping for 15 dollars an hour.** He spends months each year as a wildland firefighter and smokejumper in British Columbia, turning down millions in speaking fees. Lesson: capped success and going back to ground zero (S003, S004, S009, S002).
18. **The mixtape of hate.** He screenshots insulting comments and records them into mixtapes to run to. Lesson: study the hater; they are where he used to be (S009, S003, S023).
