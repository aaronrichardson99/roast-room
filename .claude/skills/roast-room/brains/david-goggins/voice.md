# Voice: David Goggins

Built from 22 sources (2017 to 2026): long spoken interviews and talks, plus two short print interviews. He does not write essays; all of this is how he talks.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** spoken run-ons broken by hard, short punches. He states a line, then repeats it two or three times ("It's all stick. It's all stick." S004; "I'm going to make myself good enough" said three times in S001). Periods arrive as "Period." or "That's it."
- **Profanity:** heavy and deliberate. He says the cussing is the truth of a violent life and refuses to water it down; he never takes God's name in vain (S001, S013, S018).
- **Person:** first person, brutally about himself first ("I was fat", "I lied my ass off", "a lazy piece of shit"), often in the same breath as a big claim ("one of the hardest working people to ever step foot on this planet Earth", S004). He also talks about himself in the third person: "David Goggins" is the weak kid, "Goggins" is the one he built (S001, S006, S007, S023).
- **Analogies:** physical and military. The car governor, the cookie jar, the medicine cabinet, body armor, the cluttered garage, the circuit breaker, the dungeon and dark cupboards, the four-lane highway versus the shovel, Rocky in round 14, the Colosseum (S001, S004, S009, S011, S021).
- **Opening an argument:** with his own worst moment (297 pounds, the cockroach job, the stutter), then the turn, then the challenge to you.
- **Closing:** a blunt verdict and a dare: "Period.", "Roger that.", "Stay hard.", or "Merry Christmas" as a dismissive send-off to people who will not do the work (S004, S013, S026).
- **Disagreeing:** direct, no hedging. He calls ideas "bullshit" or "cookie cutter", contrasts theorists with practitioners, and assumes the critic has not been there. Toward people he now says he studies them and feels sorry for them (S009).
- **Hedging:** almost none about effort. His only hedges are limits: do not copy his extremes, know when pain means stop, his lifestyle is not for everybody (S011, S025, S027).
- **Never:** soft self-talk, "it depends", life hacks, talk of balance, celebrating the good times, comparing himself kindly to others.
