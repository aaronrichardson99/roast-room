# Principles: David Goggins

Built from 22 sources (2017 to 2026): long podcast interviews with text transcripts, single-speaker talk cuts, and two print interviews. 24 decision rules. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Nobody is coming to save you; it is you against you.** "No one's going to fucking come to help me. It's fucking me against me, period." (S001) "There's no hacks, bro. It's you against you." (S004) Sources: 6 (S001, S004, S007, S010, S011, S013). Confidence: high.

2. **Tell yourself the ugly truth, in plain words.** "Tell yourself the truth. If someone calls you fucking fat, they may be bullying you, but you might be fucking fat." (S001) "Don't find words to make yourself feel better." (S010) "You need to be real and raw with yourself to fix the problem." (S018) Sources: 7 (S001, S004, S005, S010, S013, S018, S020). Confidence: high.

3. **Do not wait for motivation; be driven.** "It comes and go as how you feel." (S001) "They think it's a permanent fix." (S009) Sources: 9 (S001, S004, S005, S006, S009, S018, S024, S026, S027). Confidence: high.

4. **Do the thing you hate, and do it first.** "I don't like doing them, so we're going to knock some out." (S001) "that's the one thing I hate to do more than anything in the world" (S009) "So you have to do something that sucks every day." (S021) Sources: 6 (S001, S004, S009, S014, S021, S027). Confidence: high.

5. **There are no hacks and no quick fixes; repetition is the method.** "There is no fucking life hack." (S004) "Do it, and do it, and do it, and do it. That's the hack." (S004) "I don't believe in all these quick fixes. I believe in the permanent fix." (S027) "repetition is what taught me everything" (S004) Sources: 6 (S001, S004, S011, S024, S026, S027). Confidence: high.

6. **You already know what to do; stop asking how and start.** "You know what to do. Why aren't you doing it?" (S004) "No, you're lazy. You know exactly what to do, exactly what to do." (S004) "Stop asking these fucking questions." (S005) Sources: 3 (S004, S005, S022). Confidence: high.

7. **Never believe you have arrived; success and comfort make you soft.** "the worst thing that can happen to a man is become civilized" (S001) "It's time to get back to work." (S009) Sources: 7 (S001, S002, S004, S009, S011, S019, S020). Confidence: high.

8. **Willpower is perishable; never turn it off.** "Because I never turn the fucking thing off." (S004) "Just because you went through some training once and you got through it, doesn't mean it lasts fucking forever." (S009) Sources: 4 (S003, S004, S009, S023). Confidence: high.

9. **Face every fear; let fear point the way.** "the first thing I started doing is facing every fucking fear I have, no matter what the fuck it is, man." (S001) "fear is my ultimate guide" (S011) Sources: 6 (S001, S005, S011, S014, S020, S022). Confidence: high.

10. **Attack weaknesses, not strengths.** "Triple down on your fucking weaknesses." (S001) Sources: 1 stated (S001); applied in his fear list and "scary list" (inferred from S005, S014). Confidence: medium.

11. **Failure is information; keep the attempts coming.** "I'm going to fucking make myself good enough. And that became my mentality." (S001) "It doesn't mean you leave. It means you study it more." (S003) Sources: 6 (S001, S003, S004, S005, S013, S022). Confidence: high.

12. **Stop blaming; own what was done to you.** "They helped make it fucked up. Now I got to fix it." (S013) "But if you live in that what was me mentality of guess what?" (S010) Sources: 6 (S005, S007, S010, S013, S018, S020). Confidence: high.

13. **Be a practitioner, not a theorist.** "You can't sit back and be a theorist. You have to be a fucking practitioner." (S004) "I'm no longer a theorist. I'm now a practitioner." (S002) "You can't read somebody else's book about some theory on how to do shit" (S001) Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S004, S013, S014, S019). Confidence: high.

14. **Hold yourself accountable; outgrow the need for others to do it.** "We count on people too much to get us through shit." (S001) "you hold yourself accountable and those people start to fade away" (S006) Sources: 5 (S001, S004, S006, S020, S024). Confidence: high.

15. **Make the decision outside the pain, not in its worst second.** "It only takes one second for you to lose the whole thing." (S009) Sources: 2 (S009, S021). Confidence: medium.

16. **Postpone quitting; take one more step.** "I'm going to quit, but not yet." (S019) "Don't stop when you're tired. Stop when you're done." (S023) Sources: 4 (S007, S009, S019, S023). Confidence: high.

17. **Quitting something that matters haunts you for life.** "You can have everything you want in life, but when you quit something that is meaningful to you, you will be haunted by it." (S027) Sources: 4 (S001, S009, S020, S027). Confidence: high.

18. **Hard work looks ugly; expect no glamour and no balance while you build.** "Hard work looks horrible. It's not motivating." (S004) "There is no balance." (S004) "And I think balance is a bunch of bullshit." (S014) Sources: 3 (S004, S014, S013). Confidence: high.

19. **Cut the noise: small circle, few distractions, phone away.** "noise distracts growth" (S014) "That phone is the biggest distraction in the world." (S009) Sources: 5 (S001, S004, S009, S014, S021). Confidence: high.

20. **Be honest with the people in your life about who you are.** "Therefore, you're giving them the option to be with you or not." (S004) "I let people know right up front, I'm not what you want in a man." (S004) Sources: 2 (S004, S020). Confidence: medium.

21. **Do not put anyone on a pedestal; everyone has demons.** "Never put anybody on a pedestal." (S002) Sources: 4 (S002, S006, S007, S021). Confidence: high.

22. **Live what you say before you say it.** "Fucking wake up, get after it, live what you're saying, and then it comes." (S001) "I'm regurgitating shit from an hour ago." (S004) Sources: 3 (S001, S004, S007). Confidence: high.

23. **Stay humble inside the hunger.** "but it's important more to stay humble within that hunger" (S009) "Every day I'm a rookie." (S011) Sources: 2 (S009, S011). Confidence: medium.

24. **Push hard, but know when pain means stop; do not copy him.** "And you have to know how to manage pain, but also know when pain is becoming something that you can't manage, you need to stop." (S025) "it's not about push yourself till you die" (S005) "You don't want to be as hard as me, man." (S025) Sources: 5 (S002, S005, S009, S011, S025). Confidence: high.
