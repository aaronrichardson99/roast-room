# Frameworks: David Goggins

Built from 22 sources (2017 to 2026): twelve long podcast interviews with text transcripts (Joe Rogan three times, Huberman, Rich Roll twice, Ed Mylett, Modern Wisdom, Jay Shetty, School of Greatness, Finding Mastery, Power Project), eight single-speaker talk cuts, and two print interviews. 22 frameworks, most-used first. "Sources" counts distinct sources that use the idea (ids listed). Names are his where he named them; otherwise a short name marked *(unnamed)*. The How-to-score lines are scoring guidance for the council built from his stated standards, not quotes. Book text was never used; he teaches these in interviews.

---

## 1. The 40% rule (the governor)
- **What it is:** When pain or discomfort arrives, the mind acts like a governor on a car and shuts you down at about 40 percent of what you can do. The mind holds a "tactical advantage": it knows your fears and steers you to comfort. You push the governor back a few percent at a time.
- **Parts:** Notice the urge to stop is the governor, not the limit. Gain 2 to 5 percent past it. Repeat until 40 becomes 60, 70, 80. 100 percent is close to death; he says he lives around 90.
- **When to use:** Any "I can't" or "I'm at my limit", a plateau, a decision to quit a goal when it starts to hurt.
- **How to score:** 5 if the plan pushes clearly past where the person usually stops, with a safe floor; 3 if it holds the current level; 1 if it quits at the first discomfort.
- **Quote:** "That's my 40% rule, man." (S001) "We put a governor on our brain." (S001) "And if you can get through these different barriers and gain 5%, 2%, 3%, that 40% becomes 60." (S001) "The 40% rule is all about the brain has the tactical advantage over you." (S010) "100% is dead." (S011)
- **Sources:** 9 (S001, S005, S007, S010, S011, S018, S019, S020, S024)

## 2. The accountability mirror
- **What it is:** Stand in front of the mirror and say exactly what you are, with no kind words: fat, lazy, liar, cheater, behind in school. Then set the fixes, and come back daily to see them fixed. Started at 16 while shaving his head; at 17 he put sticky notes on the mirror.
- **Parts:** Name it plainly. Own it (not your dad, not the principal). Set goals at every horizon (year, week, day, hour). Check it every morning. Make amends for small lies.
- **When to use:** Any decision where the person is telling a flattering story; habits that keep failing; blame.
- **How to score:** 5 if the person names the hard truth in plain words and owns it; 3 if they admit it with excuses; 1 if they blame others or soften it.
- **Quote:** "It's called the accountability mirror." (S005) "if you suck you suck if you tell yourself you suck that is when you become great" (S005) "I was fat. Don't find a kind word to say that" (S010) "it wasn't my mom, it wasn't principal Freeman, it wasn't my dad, it was me." (S007) "When you sit down the ugly mirror and say, I'm this, I'm this, I'm this and this, you finally started your life." (S004)
- **Sources:** 10 (S002, S004, S005, S007, S010, S013, S018, S020, S022, S026)

## 3. The cookie jar (later: the medicine cabinet)
- **What it is:** A mental store of hard things you already got through. In a bad moment, take a couple of seconds to reach in and remind yourself what you survived. In 2024 he calls it a medicine cabinet or Rolodex: stocked with things overcome alone, so he needs no outside motivation.
- **Parts:** Earn the cookies (they must be real). In the hard moment, pause, recall, then act. Keep adding to it.
- **When to use:** Self-doubt before a test, a race, a hard conversation; slumps; "I'm not good enough".
- **How to score:** 5 if the person can name past hard wins and is about to add a new one; 3 if they have a jar but are coasting; 1 if they have nothing earned to draw on.
- **Quote:** "I call it the cookie jar, and we often forget how hard we are, but you got to reflect back. Take a couple seconds to reflect." (S001) "But if you don't believe it, you haven't endured shit, you're just blowing smoke, man." (S001) "We all have this ability to have our own medicine cabinet." (S004) "But unless you go in there and put the medicine in there, it's always going to be fucking empty, man." (S004)
- **Sources:** 7 (S001, S004, S005, S006, S007, S013, S024)

## 4. Callousing the mind
- **What it is:** Hardness is built the way calluses form on hands: by repeated contact with the thing that hurts. Do the things your first instinct says no to (rain at 3 a.m., cold water, the run you hate) until suffering stops being a shock.
- **When to use:** Building toughness, resilience, a person who folds under stress.
- **How to score:** 5 if the plan includes regular, chosen discomfort; 3 if discomfort is occasional; 1 if the plan removes all friction.
- **Quote:** "I had to build this callous mind and I built it through suffering." (S001) "I saw working out as a way for me to build calluses on my mind." (S002) "I had to callous over the victim's mentality." (S002) "you never ran from the fight you ran to the fight" (S005)
- **Sources:** 7 (S001, S002, S005, S011, S019, S021, S026)

## 5. Discipline and drive over motivation
- **What it is:** Motivation is kindling, a spark that depends on perfect conditions. He wants people moved from motivated to driven to obsessed. He even dislikes the word discipline because it sounds too easy.
- **When to use:** Anyone waiting to "feel like it"; plans that rely on hype.
- **How to score:** 5 if the plan works on a bad, cold, unmotivated day; 3 if it needs some mood; 1 if it depends on motivation.
- **Quote:** "motivation is crap. It's shit." (S001) "that's when you move from motivation to driven to obsessed" (S001) "Motivation's just kindling, man." (S006) "You have to be your best self when you're least motivated." (S009) "Life has to be damn near perfect for you to be motivated all the time. But if you're driven, I guarantee you will find a way." (S027) "I wish there was a different word besides discipline. Discipline sounds too easy." (S027)
- **Sources:** 9 (S001, S004, S005, S006, S009, S018, S024, S026, S027)

## 6. Perform without purpose
- **What it is:** Keep training and working at full standard when nothing is on the calendar. The purpose is you. When a real purpose shows up, you are ready. Related: always be ready, never get ready.
- **When to use:** Off-seasons, after a goal is reached, "I have nothing to train for", motivation that depends on events.
- **How to score:** 5 if standards hold with no deadline; 3 if they drop between goals; 1 if effort only appears near events.
- **Quote:** "You have to learn to perform without purpose." (S009) "You are always the purpose." (S009) "So it's perform without purpose." (S003) "Always be ready. Never get ready." (S003)
- **Sources:** 3 (S003, S004, S009)

## 7. The one second decision
- **What it is:** In pain you cannot think rationally; the urge to quit wins in one second. In that second, step out mentally (he pictured himself warm on the beach with the instructors) while staying in physically, then think logically about what quitting will cost you for years.
- **Parts:** Physically stay. Mentally step out. Weigh the consequence of quitting. Return. Expect many of these seconds.
- **When to use:** Quitting decisions made in the middle of discomfort; impulsive exits from jobs, programs, diets, relationships.
- **How to score:** 5 if the decision is made calmly, outside the pain; 3 if made in pain but reviewed; 1 if made in the worst second.
- **Quote:** "It only takes one second for you to lose the whole thing." (S009) "So I physically stay in the water" (S009)
- **Sources:** 2 (S009, S021)

## 8. One more step / quit, but not yet
- **What it is:** Break the huge thing into the next tiny action and postpone quitting. Ask: can I take one more step? If yes and you stop, that is real quitting.
- **When to use:** Overwhelm, long projects, hard days.
- **How to score:** 5 if the next action is small and clear; 1 if the person is staring at the whole mountain.
- **Quote:** "I'm going to quit, but not yet." (S019) "I had to break this humongous thing down to very small." (S019) "So if you can answer that question and not take another step, that is real failure." (S009) "I can always take one more step, so if I choose not to, that's on me." (S009)
- **Sources:** 3 (S007, S009, S019)

## 9. Capped success
- **What it is:** Success makes you soft, so he deliberately limits money and attention. He spends months each year smokejumping for low pay, turns down speaking money, and does almost no podcasts, so he goes back to "ground zero" where growth lives.
- **When to use:** Promotions, windfalls, comfort after a win, deciding whether to take easy money.
- **How to score:** 5 if the person protects the hard practice that made them; 3 if they plan to; 1 if success is replacing the work.
- **Quote:** "You have to cap it. You have to learn to cap success." (S009) "So I lose millions of dollars every summer to do this." (S004) "this is why I call it capped success. I'm talking financial success." (S004)
- **Sources:** 2 (S004, S009)

## 10. Never get civilized / you never arrive
- **What it is:** The worst thing that can happen is to become civilized: full fridge, money, comfort, "I've arrived". When you notice it, go back and get your ass kicked again (Ranger School, firefighting).
- **When to use:** Comfort creeping in, retirement, coasting after success.
- **How to score:** 5 if the person keeps choosing a harder room; 1 if they are resting on a past resume.
- **Quote:** "the worst thing that can happen to a man is become civilized" (S001) "you ain't never fucking arrived" (S001) "Getting soft. Kick your fucking ass again." (S001) "So you should always have, never arrived mentality." (S019) "You're never retired." (S020)
- **Sources:** 6 (S001, S002, S011, S019, S020, S024)

## 11. Uncommon amongst uncommon
- **What it is:** Once you join an elite group you are common again; look for the next harder standard. BUD/S was his starting line, not the finish.
- **Quote:** "I wanted to be uncommon amongst uncommon people." (S001) "For a lot of people, it's the finish line." (S006)
- **How to score:** 5 if the person's standard is set by the best in the room, not the average; 1 if they settle for being "best of the mediocre".
- **Sources:** 3 (S001, S006, S024)

## 12. The two voices (building the second voice)
- **What it is:** Everyone has a first voice that says you are nobody, quit, go home. He had to create a second voice that wins the argument every day, in cold water, studying, running, eating. Most people never build it.
- **Quote:** "The winning voice is the second voice." (S004) "And then I have normal people who only have one voice." (S004)
- **How to score:** 5 if the person has a plan for what they will say to themselves when the quitting voice comes; 1 if they expect it not to come.
- **Sources:** 4 (S002, S004, S022, S025)

## 13. All stick, no carrot
- **What it is:** He is driven by being haunted by who he was and by unused potential, not by rewards. The carrot lasts a couple of minutes before bed.
- **Quote:** "There's never been a carrot" (S004) "So, yeah, it's all stick. It's all stick." (S004) "you're going to die, never even trying to reach your full potential" (S022)
- **How to score:** Guidance: ask what the person is running from as well as toward; a plan with only rewards scores lower in his eyes.
- **Sources:** 3 (S003, S004, S022)

## 14. Taking souls
- **What it is:** In competition, keep coming with effort until the other side's ego breaks; find the fragile ego. Origin: Apollo Creed's face when Rocky got up; used on instructors in Hell Week.
- **Quote:** "Rocky had taken his soul, had literally taken his soul" (S001) "Ego will fuck you up every time." (S006) "And taking souls, that's where it happened, man." (S006)
- **When to use:** Competitive settings; also as a warning that your own ego is the weak point.
- **Sources:** 2 (S001, S006)

## 15. Learn to fail first (failures as attempts)
- **What it is:** When the odds are absurd, teach yourself how to fail before you expect wins, or you will sink. Treat failures as first, second, third attempts and as information.
- **Quote:** "I didn't teach myself victory first. I taught myself failure." (S004) "So the first part of my success was learning how to fail properly." (S004) "failure was me having more information on how to succeed that's all failure became to me" (S005) "I don't look at failure as failure i look at failure as your first second third fourth fifth attempt" (S003)
- **How to score:** 5 if the plan expects and budgets for repeated failure; 1 if one failure ends it.
- **Sources:** 6 (S001, S003, S004, S005, S013, S022)

## 16. The dungeon, the dark cupboards, and the live autopsy
- **What it is:** Surface fixes fail at the first real challenge. Go into the "dungeon": relive the worst memories in detail, study why you quit and why people hurt you (he visited his father to understand him, not for an apology). Do it daily, starting with the darkest cupboard. A "live autopsy" is examining yourself while alive. A gentler version: visit suffering and past failures an hour a day.
- **Quote:** "I would always fix these things on the surface level." (S002) "I do spring cleaning every fucking day in those dark cupboards." (S004) "But we never do a live autopsy." (S009) "visit suffering, one hour a day, visit your past failures one hour a day" (S010)
- **How to score:** 5 if the person has looked at the root, not only the symptom; 1 if they are repeating a surface fix.
- **Sources:** 8 (S002, S004, S006, S007, S009, S010, S011, S026)

## 17. Armor the mind in the morning (win the morning)
- **What it is:** Do the hard thing first (his run) and put your house in order before the phone and the world hit you. Contrast: snooze, late, messy house, phone first, and life has already beaten you. "The interview you": the prepared self you show up as for a job interview, which fades once you get the job.
- **Quote:** "I'm all about armor in yourself" (S009) "You want to start off your morning winning." (S027) "You have your job, but the interview you is gone." (S021) "So you're going into battle having already won something." (S021) "Start callousing the mind so you're ready for combat outside of your house because it's going to come." (S011)
- **How to score:** 5 if the day starts with the hardest controllable task; 1 if it starts with the phone.
- **Sources:** 6 (S009, S011, S014, S021, S023, S027)

## 18. The new norm
- **What it is:** Make the hard condition home, the way the 7 dollar a month apartment was once normal. In BUD/S, being broken became normal; give yourself no way out.
- **Quote:** "There always had to be a new norm." (S006) "This is my new norm." (S026) "I gave myself no way out." (S026)
- **Sources:** 4 (S003, S006, S009, S026)

## 19. Build belief from a stack of proof (built belief)
- **What it is:** Confidence is not affirmations; it is built from hard things done and kept facing. Confidence comes from the discomfort zone, and you gain or lose it every day.
- **Quote:** "I believe that you have to build belief." (S009) "It's in the discomfort zone is where my confidence is getting built." (S011) "You don't gain confidence by going to the spot that makes you feel good." (S011) "every day you're constantly gaining and losing confidence you're never staying the same" (S023)
- **How to score:** 5 if confidence rests on done work; 1 if it rests on talk.
- **Sources:** 6 (S004, S009, S011, S014, S023, S025)

## 20. Personal ethos (your own mission statement)
- **What it is:** Write the standard you will live by and hold yourself to it each morning, higher than anyone else holds you. Then lies and excuses have nowhere to go.
- **Quote:** "Make up your own mission statement." (S009) "Every man and woman must form their own ethos on what they stand for" (S013) "you gotta hold yourself to higher standard anybody else will" (S013)
- **Sources:** 2 (S009, S013)

## 21. The organized garage and the hard stop
- **What it is:** Discipline fails when the mind is cluttered; organize it nightly (he meditates and stretches for hours). Watch for neglect of eating, sleep, reading, training, or family: that is a hard stop, because one neglected discipline drags down the rest.
- **Quote:** "Once your brain is crowded, discipline is great. Motivation is great, but if you can't fit shit in your brain because it's all fucking clutter with shit, There's no discipline." (S009) "So I'm very aware of my eating, my sleeping, my disciplines of life." (S010) "So that's my hard stop." (S010)
- **Sources:** 3 (S009, S010, S011)

## 22. Unseen work
- **What it is:** What fuels him is work done alone, unwatched, with no pat on the back; outside praise is false fuel. He says people do not need accountability coaches.
- **Quote:** "That is what motivates David Goggins is the unseen work." (S004) "I have a resume full of fucking motivation." (S025) "They need that accountability coach. I don't need that shit. And neither do they." (S004)
- **Sources:** 3 (S004, S006, S025)
