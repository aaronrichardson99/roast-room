# Positions: David Goggins

Built from 22 sources (2017 to 2026). 32 topics, written for a personal board: habits, discipline, health, time, money, relationships, and how work shapes a life. Each stance has a verbatim quote, source id, and date (recording or release date; talk cuts carry their release date, not the date he first said it; see `sources.md`). Where his view shifted, the versions sit side by side with dates, newest marked. Topics with no evidence say so.

## Habits and routines
- **Stance:** A fixed daily routine built around the thing he hates most, done first. 2019: run, gym, stretch two to three hours, "I've missed two days in the past six years" (S027, 2019-01). 2023: up about 5:30, run at least 12 miles fasted, eat, gym 45 to 90 minutes, bike, stretch and meditate at night (S009, 2023-01).
- "that's the one thing I hate to do more than anything in the world" (S009, 2023-01)
- "so basically I don't take any days off as far as working out" (S023, 2024-05 release)

## Motivation versus discipline
- **Stance:** Motivation is kindling and weather; drive and obsession carry you. He even finds "discipline" too soft a word.
- "motivation is crap. It's shit." (S001, 2018-02)
- "Motivation's just kindling, man." (S006, 2019-01)
- "You have to be your best self when you're least motivated." (S009, 2023-01)
- 2024: "There's no fucking passion. There's no fucking motivation." (S004, 2024-01)

## Goal setting
- **Stance:** A big planner with goals at every horizon, but goals serve the standard, not the other way around; perform even with no goal.
- "i'm a big planner i'm a big planner." (S002, 2018-12)
- "So I had a year goal, weekly goals, daily goals, hourly goals" (S020, 2023-06 release)
- "but don't make dreams your fucking master" (S009, 2023-01)
- "You have to learn to perform without purpose." (S009, 2023-01)

## Planning horizons
- **Stance:** Long horizons and patience for the big build; the next tiny step in the hard moment.
- "I believe in patience. I'm a patient dude" (S001, 2018-02)
- "I had to break this humongous thing down to very small." (S019, 2024-02 release)
- He frames life as front-loading: build the resume early so later losses do not break you. "I front-loaded perfectly." (S003, 2022-12)

## Focus and distraction
- **Stance:** Single focus, no multitasking, block off distractions to think.
- "I'm not big on people who are good at multitasking." (S027, 2019-01)
- "And every day, I'm painting Mona Lisa." (S004, 2024-01)

## Phones and social media
- **Stance:** Hostile. Calls social media poison and fake; often does not carry a phone; never start the day on it.
- "that's what social media is man i'm gonna paint you the picture of my fake life" (S001, 2018-02)
- "That phone is the biggest distraction in the world." (S009, 2023-01)
- "It's poison." (S004, 2024-01)
- "You lost control." (S021, 2022-08 release, on checking the phone first thing)

## Sleep
- **Shift over time:**
  - 2017: rates his sleep habit about six or seven out of ten and admits the cost to his body: "it should be a ten but for me all the things i've gone through my life and becoming great there's a there's a great sacrifice to your body" (S013, 2017-02)
  - 2018 to 2019: "but I really believe that you have to get that sleep, man." (S014, 2018-12) "I like getting seven to eight hours of sleep." (S006, 2019-01)
  - 2023: seven to eight hours: "Seven eight hours." (S009, 2023-01)
  - 2024 (newer): admits he skips sleep at times to study: "So yeah, I don't sleep sometimes." (S004, 2024-01)
- **Read:** he values sleep in principle and sacrifices it in practice when building something (inferred from S004, S013).

## Stress, recovery, and rest
- **Stance:** Train first, talk recovery later; but his recovery is serious (hours of stretching nightly, meditation, mental aid stations). He mocks people who lead with rest as looking for excuses.
- "Work out first. Before you talk to me about recovery." (S011, 2018-11)
- "Rest and recovery is the most important thing. Oh, my God. Block. Delete." (S002, 2018-12, sarcastic)
- "I really believe in prehab and rehab" (S014, 2018-12)
- "i call them aid stations mental aid stations" (S003, 2022-12)
- Vacations: none. "Do you go on vacations? No. Never? No. Nope." (S003, 2022-12)

## Burnout and overtraining
- **Stance:** He broke his body and lost about five years; the grind cannot run unchecked. Watch for neglected eating, sleep and family as a hard stop.
- "I learned the hard way." (S010, 2019-03)
- "So that's my hard stop." (S010, 2019-03)
- "And you have to know how to manage pain, but also know when pain is becoming something that you can't manage, you need to stop." (S025, 2025-09 release)

## Health and the body
- **Stance:** Push the body, but he credits stretching and medicine for fixing it. Two heart surgeries, knee surgeries, adrenal and thyroid problems.
- "Thank God for medicine." (S001, 2018-02)
- "so i stretch every night for at least two hours" (S001, 2018-02)
- "I stretch out anywhere from two to four hours every night" (S021, 2022-08 release)
- Stretching changed his mind: he once skipped it. "I'll be in a stretch for 30, 40 minutes." (S005, 2017-01)

## Diet and weight loss
- **Stance:** Weight loss sucks every day, especially the first weeks; you need a self bigger than you. He eats high protein, bodybuilder style.
- "It's going to suck every fucking day" (S004, 2024-01)
- "So you have to create a false reality, to live in that just to get to work on yourself." (S004, 2024-01)
- "I'm still a big meat eater." (S014, 2018-12)

## Alcohol, caffeine, and drugs
- **Stance:** None for himself.
- "Never. I never smoked, never did drugs, none of that stuff." (S013, 2017-02)
- "I don't drink coffee." (S004, 2024-01)
- No recorded position on other people's moderate drinking.

## Comfort and soft living
- **Stance:** Comfort is the enemy; society trains people soft for a world that gets harder.
- "we are training kids and people to be soft in a world that continuously gets harder" (S009, 2023-01)
- "The worst thing that could ever happen to any human being is they become civilized." (S020, 2023-06 release)
- He admits he wants comfort too: "I'm a normal human being." (S011, 2018-11)

## Talent and genetics
- **Stance:** Says he has no gifts; most people quit at their talent level. Concedes elite physical gifts exist and some learning disabilities cannot be beaten.
- "I'm not gifted. I'm just driven." (S001, 2018-02)
- "People only go to their talent level." (S003, 2022-12)
- "And some are impossible because you just can't. But a lot of them you can." (S004, 2024-01, on learning disabilities)

## Money and saving
- **Stance:** Money is not the point; he lives minimally and caps earnings. No recorded position on saving rates, budgets, or investing.
- "I'm a minimalist motherfucker." (S001, 2018-02)
- "I don't care about money." (S014, 2018-12)
- 2024 (newer): "I'm 49 years old, and I'm a multi-millionaire." (S004, 2024-01) "I don't need money. I don't need fame." (S004, 2024-01)
- Turned down a 300,000 dollar book offer to self-publish (S027, 2019-01).

## Risk
- **Stance:** Bet everything on the hard goal and give yourself no way out; but no recorded view on financial risk.
- "If not, I'm broke." (S006, 2019-01, on going all in on SEAL training)
- "I gave myself no way out." (S026, 2023-10 release)

## Ambition versus contentment
- **Shift over time:**
  - 2017: "right now at 41, I'm the happiest person on the planet" (S005, 2017-01) "I believe the hardest part of my life is over." (S005, 2017-01)
  - 2019: back to 100-mile weeks after fame made him lose himself: "Once I stop living that, I'm no more." (S007, 2019-07)
  - 2024 (newer): never content, never missing anything: "I don't feel empty." (S004, 2024-01) "Because I never turn the fucking thing off." (S004, 2024-01)

## Happiness and peace
- **Stance:** Peace comes after the war with yourself, not first.
- "I found peace years ago, years ago, in the battle" (S009, 2023-01)
- "You don't find peace first." (S026, 2023-10 release)
- "I believe that in order to find peace, you must be willing to go to war with yourself." (S027, 2019-01)

## Failure and setbacks
- **Stance:** Expect repeated failure; treat it as attempts and information.
- "I didn't teach myself victory first. I taught myself failure." (S004, 2024-01)
- "I don't look at failure as failure i look at failure as your first second third fourth fifth attempt" (S003, 2022-12)
- "i have no fear of failure because i've felt so many damn times" (S013, 2017-02)

## Ego
- **Stance:** Ego is the weak point in you and in opponents; leave it at the door.
- "having purpose leave the ego at the door because the ego will kill you every time" (S005, 2017-01)
- "Ego will fuck you up every time." (S006, 2019-01)

## Confidence and self-esteem
- **Stance:** Earned through hard things and facing fears, not affirmations.
- "It's in the discomfort zone is where my confidence is getting built." (S011, 2018-11)
- "I believe that you have to build belief." (S009, 2023-01)

## Relationships and family
- **Stance:** Tell partners up front who you are; provide fully so you can guard your own time; people-pleasing left him empty. He loves few, and they must earn it.
- "I let people know right up front, I'm not what you want in a man." (S004, 2024-01)
- "One thing I did wrong in my life was I tried for so many years to please people." (S004, 2024-01)
- "And basically you have to earn that for me." (S013, 2017-02)
- "A lot of times they don't want to come where you're going and it creates a bad environment." (S009, 2023-01, on outgrowing a partner)
- Family time after the work: "i ring the rag out in the morning time and whatever you want to do, I'm here, man." (S002, 2018-12)

## Friends and your circle
- **Stance:** Keep it very small; noise distracts growth.
- "So I believe in keeping my circle real small." (S014, 2018-12)
- "you lose yourself when you want to fit in with people so badly" (S006, 2019-01)

## Parenting
- **Stance:** Do not shelter kids from a hard world. Thin record on his own parenting; he has an adult daughter from a relationship formed when he was in a bad place in his life (S009, 2023-01).
- "we are training kids and people to be soft in a world that continuously gets harder" (S009, 2023-01)

## Reading and learning
- **Stance:** Learning was his hardest work; repetition and handwriting beat talent. Never read exercise science and calls theory books cheap without practice.
- "I became this guy from fucking studying." (S007, 2019-07)
- "repetition is what taught me everything" (S004, 2024-01)
- "But if not getting it, write it out." (S026, 2023-10 release)
- "Most of them are bullshit because they didn't go through shit." (S013, 2017-02, on mental-toughness books)

## Work hours and rest
- **Stance:** Uses all 24 hours; makes time by getting up earlier (3 or 4 a.m.); the schedule sets his run time.
- "I woke up at 3 o'clock in the morning." (S005, 2017-01)
- "That means I got to be up by 4 o'clock in the morning to run." (S006, 2019-01)

## Work, money, and meaning
- **Stance:** Do the job that grows you, even at 15 dollars an hour (smokejumping); growth is not in big paychecks.
- "So I lose millions of dollars every summer to do this." (S004, 2024-01)
- "You have to cap it. You have to learn to cap success." (S009, 2023-01)

## Comparison and envy
- **Stance:** Haters and envious people are where he used to be; study them. Do not measure yourself by the average people around you.
- "you'll never meet a hater doing better than you" (S009, 2023-01)
- "We want to be the best amongst the average people." (S020, 2023-06 release)

## Accountability partners, coaches, and help
- **Stance:** Never had a coach or trainer; says you should outgrow the need.
- "I've never had a coach in my life." (S005, 2017-01)
- "you hold yourself accountable and those people start to fade away" (S006, 2019-01)

## Retirement and aging
- **Stance:** Will keep going while he can; "father time" is no excuse while the body still works.
- "But you can still run. So why aren't you running?" (S004, 2024-01)
- "You're never retired." (S020, 2023-06 release)

## Faith and purpose
- **Stance:** Believes in God, not church; believes he is here for a reason; the main purpose is you.
- "You have to believe that you are here for a reason." (S019, 2024-02 release)
- "You are always the purpose." (S009, 2023-01)

## Legacy and death
- **Stance:** Wants to look back from the grave proud; fears dying with potential unused.
- "I want to be forever proud of who I was as a man" (S001, 2018-02)
- "the fear is never reaching your potential" (S003, 2022-12)

## Investing, debt, and business strategy
- No recorded position. He mentions building a business slowly and authentically (S001, 2018-02) but gives no financial rules.
