# Principles: Brad Schoenfeld

22 decision rules, built from 45 sources (blog 2010 to 2024, interviews 2010 to 2024, papers 2010 to 2023). "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **No cookie-cutter answers; every applied answer starts with "it depends".** "virtually my answer to any applied question is gonna be, it depends" (S002) "There are no cookie-cutter prescriptions for getting big." (S013) Sources: 7 (S001, S002, S005, S006, S013, S010, S011). Confidence: high.

2. **Context decides whether an effect matters.** "The difference in getting an extra pound of muscle over time probably has little relevance to a stockbroker or a insurance salesperson" (S001) Sources: 6 (S001, S004, S008, S010, S014, S005). Confidence: high.

3. **Adherence beats the optimal plan.** "I could give someone the best diet in the world if they hate the foods that they’re eating, it’s not gonna work." (S002) "Remember: The best workout routine is the one that you’re able to stick with." (S013) Sources: 7 (S002, S003, S008, S009, S012, S013, S044). Confidence: high.

4. **Research reports averages; the individual must experiment.** "Use research to guide your programming, then experiment to see what works best for you." (S010) "Everyone has their own N equals one experiment." (S004) Sources: 8 (S004, S008, S010, S011, S012, S013, S003, S006). Confidence: high.

5. **One study is a piece of the puzzle, never the final word.** "one study is never the be-all-end-all when it comes to answering questions on an applied science topic." (S010) Sources: 5 (S010, S011, S020, S008, S016). Confidence: high.

6. **Acute data do not prove long-term results.** "acute findings do not necessarily translate into long-term gainz." (S008) Sources: 4 (S008, S012, S010, S036). Confidence: high.

7. **Results from untrained subjects do not transfer automatically.** "the vast majority of studies were in untrained subjects, which needs to be always taken into account" (S004) Sources: 4 (S004, S010, S011, S014). Confidence: high.

8. **No evidence is not a reason to do nothing: try what is logical and safe.** "If we don't have any good evidence to refute it and it has a sound logical basis, it is worth trying until we have a basis to refute it." (S004) "Remember: the best research often comes from what is learned in the trenches!" (S011) Sources: 4 (S004, S011, S001, S009). Confidence: high.

9. **Authority is not evidence.** "a researcher’s body of work doesn’t provide a mandate to accept his opinion as fact." (S012) "Science is about evidence." (S014) Sources: 3 (S012, S014, S020). Confidence: high.

10. **Change your mind in public when the data say so.** "I’d note the study has caused me to question my previous opinion on the implementation of deloads." (S008) "I always reserve judgment. I’m always willing to change my opinion" (S003) Sources: 6 (S003, S005, S008, S011, S012, S014). Confidence: high.

11. **Train hard; easy sets do not grow muscle.** "if you’re putting the weight down and it wasn’t challenging, then you haven’t taxed your muscles in a way that they’re gonna adapt." (S001) "the majority of general public when they go to the gym are training with two-idle intensity." (S004) Sources: 5 (S001, S004, S005, S008, S027). Confidence: high.

12. **The need for effort rises with training age.** "In novice lifters, this can be achieved stopping quite a ways away from failure; even cardio is sufficient to cause appreciable muscle growth in this population!" (S009) Sources: 3 (S001, S009, S002). Confidence: high.

13. **Beginners learn movement first.** "the most important thing is, is not focusing on those things. It’s on, it’s focusing on getting their form right." (S001) Sources: 4 (S001, S004, S005, S009). Confidence: high.

14. **Energy balance decides fat loss; hormones do not override it.** "people talk about it’s all about hormones or whatever. And that’s just silliness." (S003) "Most importantly, calories do count!" (S012) Sources: 5 (S003, S009, S012, S024, S028). Confidence: high.

15. **Get total protein right before timing.** "consuming adequate protein in combination with resistance exercise is the key factor for maximizing muscle protein accretion." (S032) Sources: 6 (S003, S005, S014, S032, S035, S036). Confidence: high.

16. **Do not design training around hormone spikes.** "a lifter shouldn’t design his workouts around trying to maximize elevations in testosterone or GH" (S006) Sources: 2 (S006, S010). Confidence: medium.

17. **Choices are rarely binary; mix methods.** "The choice doesn’t have to be binary" (S009) "But rest interval length does not have to be a binary either-or choice." (S011) Sources: 5 (S008, S009, S011, S041, S001). Confidence: high.

18. **Everything is cost and benefit.** "So again, good cost. Everything is cost benefit." (S004) Sources: 4 (S004, S015, S044, S014). Confidence: high.

19. **Safety and longevity come first.** "longevity is the most important thing in this, uh, game." (S001) "Remember that safety is paramount to any routine; an injury is bound to derail your efforts." (S013) Sources: 3 (S001, S013, S006). Confidence: high.

20. **Bro science deserves a hearing, then a test.** "People that dismiss quote unquote, bro science are silly." (S004) "In this case, it appears the bros were right…!" (S009) Sources: 3 (S004, S009, S010). Confidence: high.

21. **No shortcuts.** "Exercise doesn’t come in a pill, a pair of sneakers, or any other shortcut approach." (S019) Sources: 3 (S019, S027, S023). Confidence: high.

22. **Watch for confirmation bias, including your own.** "When scrutinizing research, it is imperative that we take an objective approach; personal bias should never enter into the analysis." (S012) "maybe it’s my bias as a, like I said, as a former bodybuilder" (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S012, S014). Confidence: high.
