# Books: Brad Schoenfeld

His books as he describes them in his blog and interviews. No book text was fetched. Summaries are in my own words.

## Science and Development of Muscle Hypertrophy (textbook, Human Kinetics, 2016; later editions)
- **Core thesis:** The first text devoted to the science of maximizing muscle growth through exercise: molecular basis, mechanisms, how each training variable affects growth, periodization models, aerobic training, and nutrition.
- **Key frameworks:** mechanical tension as the main driver with metabolic stress and damage as possible adders; volume dose response; loading across zones; frequency and rest; periodization of volume and frequency.
- **Taught in sources:** S010 and S011 (announced and described), S002 (recommended "if you're interested in the geekiness of the science").

## The M.A.X. Muscle Plan (Human Kinetics, 2012; 2.0 edition 2021)
- **Core thesis:** A six-month periodized consumer template to maximize muscle, which readers must customize to their own needs and abilities.
- **Key frameworks:** phases that move through loading zones, including a four-week metabolic phase with short rest and high reps to build buffering capacity; ends in an overreach and deload. Originally used RPE; he later told readers to use a reps-to-failure scale instead. He has said he would change its nutrient timing advice.
- **Taught in sources:** S003, S004, S011, S013, S016, S009 (foreword by John Meadows).

## Strong and Sculpted (Human Kinetics, 2016)
- **Core thesis:** A periodized program for women who want to optimize muscle development, combining evidence with field experience.
- **Key frameworks:** general and specific warm-ups, static stretching after training, periodized phases.
- **Taught in sources:** S009 (warm-up excerpt), S010, S011.

## Earlier consumer books
