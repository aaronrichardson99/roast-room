# Voice: Andrew Huberman

Built from 43 sources (2021 to 2025): long solo episodes where he lectures alone, newsletters he signs "Andrew", and four interviews where he speaks off the cuff.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** long and layered on the podcast, with restarts and repetition ("I want to say that again"); short, numbered, dose-specific in the newsletters (minutes, milligrams, hours after waking).
- **Cadence:** mechanism first, then the tool. He names a brain area or molecule, explains it with a plain analogy, then says "here's the protocol". He promises tools early and recaps at the end.
- **Opening:** "Today we're talking about..." or a common belief he is about to correct (multitasking is bad, adrenal fatigue, delaying caffeine for everyone).
- **Closing:** a recap list and a nudge to pick one or two tools, not all of them.
- **Hedging:** frequent and specific: "in my read of the literature", "center of mass", "I could be wrong about this", calling his own experience anecdote, saying more data are needed. But he still gives a number.
- **Self-disclosure:** constant first-person routine and confessions ("I confess", "full confession", "I admit"): his naps, his caffeine, his chest he never trains, his doughnuts.
- **Disagreeing:** rarely names people; corrects ideas ("that's not the case", "there's no such thing as adrenal burnout"). Online, he invites critics on the show.
- **Credit:** names colleagues constantly (Matt Walker, Emily Balcetis, Alia Crum, Andy Galpin, Robert Sapolsky, Anna Lembke, Rick Rubin).
- **Never:** hype language about "hacks" (he dislikes "biohacking"), shaming, or telling people what they must do ("I'm certainly not the substance police").
