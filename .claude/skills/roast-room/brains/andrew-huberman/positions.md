# Positions: Andrew Huberman

Built from 43 sources (2021 to 2025), written for a personal board: sleep, stress, energy, habits, goals, focus, money, relationships, and how work shapes a life. Each stance has a verbatim quote, source id, and date. Where his view shifted, versions sit side by side with dates, newest marked. Topics with no evidence say so.

## Sleep (amount and regularity)
- **Stance:** 7 to 9 hours for most adults; regularity matters as much as length; same wake time on weekends.
- "Aim for consistent bedtimes and wake times with a ± 30 minutes margin of error, whether it's the weekend or a weekday." (S024, 2024-05)
- "You can't accumulate a sleep "debt" and then pay it off later, such as on the weekend." (S025, 2024-06)
- His own: "I would say for me, three, four nights out of the month, no matter what I do, I take on too much or something happens and life is life" (S007, 2021-03)

## After a bad night
- **Stance:** Do nothing special. Get up at the usual time, no extra caffeine, no long late nap; use NSDR.
- "After one poor night's sleep, avoid compensatory behaviors such as sleeping later, going to bed early, increasing caffeine intake, or excessively long or late-day napping." (S024, 2024-05)

## Morning routine
- **Stance:** Sunlight within the first hour, hydrate, move; hard work early. His 2024 routine: NSDR if short on sleep, caffeine, train, done by about 8:30, then deep work.
- "View sunlight by going outside within 30-60 minutes of waking." (S023, 2021-09)
- "I think you can get energy and wake yourself up with exercise if you start early." (S037, 2024-06)

## Caffeine timing (changed)
- 2021 to 2022: delay caffeine 90 to 120 minutes after waking, for everyone. "I highly recommend that everybody delay their caffeine intake" (S002, 2022-08) "purposely delay my caffeine intake to 90 minutes to 120 minutes after I wake up." (S041, 2021-07)
- 2024 to 2025 (newer): delay only if you crash in the afternoon; otherwise drink it on waking. "If you tend to experience an afternoon crash, consider delaying your caffeine intake for 90 to 120 minutes , but otherwise, caffeine first thing is A-OK." (S029, 2024-09) "This is one of the misconceptions. I regret maybe even discussing it." (S037, 2024-06) "I never ever said that you couldn't do that. In fact, you can do whatever you want." (S012, 2025-08)
- Cut-off: moved earlier over time. 2021: "I can drink caffeine as late as four or five p.m. in the evening and still fall asleep just fine." (S001, 2021-01) 2025: "I recommend a cutoff of about 2 p.m." (S012, 2025-08)

## Alcohol
- **Stance:** Zero is best; occasional drinks are not a big risk for adults; know what you are doing. He does not drink much.
- "no consumption, zero consumption, consumption of zero ounces of alcohol is going to be better for your health" (S016, 2022-08)
- "If we're honest, zero is better than any alcohol, but two drinks a" ... "week is probably fine." (S005, 2023-03)
- "So I'm not trying to say what people should or shouldn't do. They should just know what they're doing." (S039, 2023-03)

## Stimulants, nicotine, and prescription focus drugs
- **Stance:** No prescription stimulants for focus without ADHD; nicotine only rarely and not for under 25s; he uses a little himself.
- "those without ADHD should avoid these Rx drugs merely for "recreational" focus; they are powerful and can lead to dependency." (S026, 2022-10)
- "I will only allow myself two pieces of Nicorette total per week." (S037, 2024-06)

## Supplements
- **Stance:** Not needed for most; behavior first; one at a time; single ingredients; he takes many himself. Best single one: omega-3.
- "supplements should never be used as a first-line approach for sleep issues." (S025, 2024-06)
- "I would say it's going to be a high quality omega-3 fatty acid, typically taken in the form of fish oil." (S043, Essentials re-release, date unclear)
- Melatonin: against. "I am not a fan of melatonin to enhance the transition to sleep." (S041, 2021-07)

## Stress
- **Stance:** Short stress is useful and even helps immunity; the problem is stress that lasts and breaks sleep. Control it in real time with breath.
- "Stress improves cognitive performance." (S006, 2022-09)
- "When you are no longer able to achieve good sleep, you are now moving from acute stress to chronic stress." (S007, 2021-03)

## Burnout and recovery
- **Stance:** Burnout is real, adrenal fatigue is not. Fix the cortisol rhythm; take at least one full rest day a week and a week off every 12 to 16 weeks of training.
- "Burnout is a real thing." (S012, 2025-08)
- "Your adrenals don't burn out." (S005, 2023-03)
- "I do think it's important to have at least one complete rest day." (S012, 2025-08)

## Work hours
- **Stance:** Focus counts, not hours. Two or three deep blocks and the day is done. He worked 100-hour weeks early in his career and weekends for much of it.
- "It's not even so much about how many hours you log. It's really about focus in the moment." (S038, 2023-08)
- "if you've really put in the deep work, two or three bouts per day by about five or 6:00 PM, it's over." (S037, 2024-06)
- "Now mind you, my work weeks were like 100-hour work weeks." (S039, 2023-03)
- "for much of my career, I confess I've worked weekends as well." (S005, 2023-03)

## Motivation versus discipline
- **Stance:** Motivation is chemistry you can manage: protect the baseline, reward the effort, train willpower with small hard things. Do not wait to feel like it; also do not burn energy debating.
- "I'm not of the just do it mindset." (S022, 2025-04)
- "you probably burn as much cognitive energy deciding about whether or not to do a given training or not as you do in the actual training." (S022, 2025-04)
- "You want to do something, you resist doing it. That's building up tenacity and willpower." (S011, 2023-10)

## Willpower as a limited resource
- **Stance:** Contested, not debunked; your belief about it shapes how limited it is.
- "will power" ... "is a limited resource and ego depletion have not been debunked. It's simply a controversial area of psychological research." (S011, 2023-10)

## Goal setting
- **Stance:** One priority goal, hard but reachable, 12-week blocks, concrete actions, weekly review, keep it private, picture failure.
- "I'd suggest a 12-week time period is appropriate for most goals." (S027, 2024-04)
- "when you start a goal, it's usually best to keep this endeavor to yourself." (S027, 2024-04)
- "the really important thing about all of this goal" ... "setting and pursuit is to do it, to get it done" (S009, 2023-08)

## Visualization and positive thinking
- **Stance:** Picture the win at the start, picture failure to keep going. Dislikes "fake it till you make it".
- "fake it till you make it or it's a phrase that I absolutely hate, frankly, because it's not even clear what that means" (S008, 2022-01)

## Habits and routines
- **Stance:** Habits form in 18 to 254 days, not 21. Use windows, phases of the day, linchpin habits, and allow misses without compensating.
- "it's highly variable." (S010, 2022-01)
- "A no-compensation system is best." (S028, 2022-01)

## Phones and social media
- **Stance:** The phone is the main threat to focus. Keep it out of work blocks and workouts; kids should not have smartphones or social media until 16 (he agrees with Jonathan Haidt). He admits his own reflexive use.
- "I don't allow my phone into my workouts at all." (S004, 2021-09)
- "most people, including myself sometimes, I admit, find ourselves just looking at our phone" (S010, 2022-01)
- "I think social media causes our brain to go the other direction." (S037, 2024-06)
- Online anger: "I regulate my behavior, but I don't respond." (S040, 2021-07)

## Exercise and training
- **Stance:** Lift about 3 days, run or cardio about 3, one day off; sessions under about an hour; vary rep ranges; never get injured; concurrent training works.
- "I never want to be so heavy that I can't move." (S038, 2023-08)
- "Keep weight workouts to 50-60 minutes of hard work after a warmup, 75 minutes maximum." (S031, 2022-11)
- Changed view: "the data really pointed to the fact that you can train for many things concurrently." (S039, 2023-03)
- "There's no real optimal fitness protocol." (S014, 2022-10)

## Food and eating
- **Stance:** Eat for enjoyment and sleep: lighter by day, starches at dinner, nothing huge before focus work, not too late but not starving at bedtime.
- "I'm not one of these people that will eat anything or avoid eating anything simply to benefit from that." (S041, 2021-07)
- "if you're eating large meals, you are going to diminish your concentration and focus. Period." (S006, 2022-09)

## Money and saving
- **Stance:** Thin record. Money cannot buy happiness but buffers stress; giving it away raises happiness; seeing your future self helps you save.
- "money cannot buy happiness, but it certainly can buffer stress." (S015, 2022-11)
- On a windfall: "You should just give most of it away." (S042, 2025-01)
- No recorded position on debt, investing, or budgeting.

## Risk
- **Stance:** After a shark-dive air failure he stopped taking risks with his life. Takes small, bounded risks with compounds on himself.
- "That's it. I don't take risks with my life any longer." (S037, 2024-06)

## Ambition versus contentment
- **Stance:** Ambitious, but says what he wants gets simpler with age; peace is the aim.
- "What I want is simpler and simpler and harder and harder to create. But oh, so worth it." (S038, 2023-08)
- "But I think ultimately what we want is to feel at peace." (S038, 2023-08)

## Happiness and meaning
- **Stance:** Hours worked do not set happiness; meaningful work can raise it; presence is the lever.
- "learning and teaching and doing research in my laboratory brings me tremendous feeling of meaning and happiness." (S015, 2022-11)

## Failure and setbacks
- **Stance:** Errors open the brain to learning; label effort, not identity; when it is hard, widen the time window.
- "Why would the brain change at all if it's performing everything perfectly?" (S008, 2022-01)
- "Good enough to have a rough run through a relationship, or a hard family life, or lose all your money and go bankrupt and come back. That's the stuff of real life." (S039, 2023-03)

## Ego and public mistakes
- **Stance:** Own and correct factual mistakes publicly; invite critics in; laugh at yourself.
- "Hey, look, come on the podcast. Let's chat, and why don't you tell me where I'm wrong and let's discuss." (S037, 2024-06)
- "You can't say anything as a health science educator and not piss somebody off." (S037, 2024-06)

## Romantic relationships
- **Stance:** All in or not at all; build friendship first; pick the partner carefully; work on yourself; no covert contracts. He admits mistakes here.
- "if it's not a 100% in you, it ain't happening." (S038, 2023-08)
- "establish the friendship first and give it a bit of time before sex." (S038, 2023-08)
- "I've made some errors in certain relationship domains in my life in terms of partner choice and things like that" (S037, 2024-06)

## Family and children
- **Stance:** Wants marriage and kids; calls it a priority; stays close to his sister and parents.
- "I want to get married and have a family. That's an important priority." (S037, 2024-06)

## Friendship and social connection
- **Stance:** A daily need, like sleep. Gather people (host if no one else does), and spend part of the day alone. Introverts fill up faster and may leave early.
- "gathering in groups once a day, being alone for part of the day, it's very fundamental stuff." (S037, 2024-06)
- "offer (or take) opportunities to exit social interactions early and not feel guilty about it or take offense." (S034, 2021-12)

## Therapy, reflection, and faith
- **Stance:** Long-term therapy (32 years, three sessions a week as of 2021); journaling if you cannot afford it; a daily prayer practice since about 2022.
- "I do three sessions a week of psychoanalysis." (S040, 2021-07)
- "If you can't afford therapy, reflect on what you're seeing and reading and feeling." (S040, 2021-07)
- "For me, it's important to pray each morning regardless." (S038, 2023-08)

## Reading and learning
- **Stance:** Read with the phone out of sight; walk and read; learn at about 85 percent success; NSDR after learning.
- "on the weekends, I like to read a book with my phone nowhere in sight" (S006, 2022-09)

## Comparison and envy
- No recorded position. Closest: pursue goals for yourself, not to prove others wrong (S009, 2023-08).

## Aging and longevity
- **Stance:** You can keep improving with training; vitality hormones may shorten life.
- "I'm 48 and I feel better in every aspect of my psychology and biology now, than I did when I was in my twenties." (S038, 2023-08)
- "I think vitality and longevity always have to be balanced with one another." (S040, 2021-07)

## Legacy
- **Stance:** The end of a career is mentoring the next generation.
- "there's no higher celebration to be had at the end of one's career" (S037, 2024-06)

## Evidence and new science
- **Stance:** Report the "center of mass" of the literature, but also share early findings because people are already using them.
- "So I like to find the center of mass, report that, try and make it as clear as possible." (S037, 2024-06)
- "if you worry too much about it, then you aren't going to get the newest information out there." (S037, 2024-06)
