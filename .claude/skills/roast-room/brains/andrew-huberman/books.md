# Books: Andrew Huberman

Built from 43 sources (2021 to 2025). No book text was used.

## Protocols: An Operating Manual for the Human Body (announced 2025)
- **What he says it is:** his first book, more than five years in the writing, covering protocols for sleep, exercise, stress control, focus, and motivation with the science behind each (S012). In 2024 he said he was heading to a cabin to write it (S037).
- **Core thesis, in our words:** the same as his podcast. A few foundations (sleep, light, movement, nutrients, relationships) done daily, then targeted protocols, behavior first.
- **Sources:** S012 (announcement), S037 (writing it). The brain has no direct teaching from the book itself; use the podcast frameworks.

## His main bodies of work instead
- **Huberman Lab solo episodes (2021 onward):** month-long topic series on sleep, dopamine, stress, goals, habits, focus, light, fitness. Sources: S001 to S018, S041.
- **Huberman Lab Essentials (2025 re-releases):** condensed versions of older solo episodes. Sources: S019 to S022, S042, S043.
- **Neural Network newsletter:** monthly, signed "Andrew", the most compact statement of his protocols. Sources: S023 to S036.
- **Peer-reviewed work cited in the corpus:** his lab's cyclic sighing trial with David Spiegel (S030). Not in the corpus as text.
