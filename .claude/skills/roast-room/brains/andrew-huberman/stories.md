# Stories: Andrew Huberman

Built from 43 sources (2021 to 2025). 16 stories and case studies he reuses, with the lesson he draws.

1. **The stacked workouts that stopped being fun.** He layered caffeine, mate, tyrosine, music, and his phone into training; workouts felt great, then his love of training and experiments faded until he cut the stack. Lesson: stacked dopamine kills intrinsic motivation. (S004, S005)
2. **The gold-star kids.** Children rewarded for drawing drew less once the rewards stopped. Lesson: external rewards for things you love can ruin them. (S005)
3. **The rat forced to run.** A rat that chose to run got healthier; a rat forced to run alongside it got sicker. Lesson: how you frame an effort changes its effect on your body. (S008, S020)
4. **The goal-line study.** People wearing ankle weights who fixed their gaze on the finish line used 17 percent less effort and got there 23 percent faster. Lesson: narrow visual focus lowers felt effort. (S008)
5. **The aged-photo savers.** People shown a digitally aged photo of themselves saved more for retirement. Lesson: make the future self visible. (S008)
6. **The $500 phone bet.** While writing grants, he handed his phone to a lab member until 5 pm; if he asked for it, everyone got $500. Lesson: build a real cost into distraction. (S009)
7. **The shark dive.** An air failure in a shark cage left him sharing air; the next day he exited the cage to face the fear, then vowed to stop risking his life. Lessons: face a trauma soon after; and life is not worth gambling. (S037, S040)
8. **The fertility math gaffe.** A clip made it look like he added probabilities to 120 percent; he posted the correct formula. Lesson: correct errors publicly and use them to teach. (S037)
9. **The cannabis critic.** A researcher attacked him on X; he invited him on the show. Lesson: online anger softens face to face; invite the critic in. (S037)
10. **Thirteen and the split.** His parents split when he was 13, his mother struggled with depression, he started therapy in secret and has kept the same therapist for decades. Lesson: invest in your mind early and keep at it. (S040)
11. **The last kid to drop in.** As a skateboarder he was fearful and anxious by default, which drove his lab's work on stress tools. Lesson: your weak spot can become your work. (S040)
12. **Costello the bulldog.** Slow morning walks for light and optic flow, walking meetings with students. Lesson: move and think together; get outside early. (S038, S041)
13. **The postdoc years.** His most stressful time, socially isolated, yet he loved the science. Lesson: stress and meaning can coexist; protect sleep through it. (S018, S039)
14. **The actor friend.** A friend with four kids told him children are the best gift you can give yourself and to choose the other parent carefully. Lesson: pick a partner with care. (S037)
15. **The windfall.** A friend with a sudden fortune asked what next; advice was to give most of it away. Lesson: blunt big rewards to keep wanting the right things. (S042)
16. **The expressive writing test.** He ranked three hard events and chose the second to try the four-session Pennebaker protocol on himself. Lesson: start with a moderate wound, stick to the protocol. (S017)
