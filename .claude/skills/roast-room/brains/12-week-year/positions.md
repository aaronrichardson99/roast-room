# Positions: Brian Moran and Michael Lennington

Built from 29 public sources (2013 to 2022). Topics are framed for a personal board: life and self-management decisions. Each stance has a verbatim quote, the author, the source id, and the date. Almost everything is Moran's; Lennington's views are marked. Their views change little over the decade covered; where tone or numbers shift, both versions are shown. Topics with no evidence say "no recorded position".

## Goal setting and New Year's resolutions
- **Stance:** Annual goals are better than none but weak. Resolutions fail because they are huge, impatient, and not believed. Replace them with 12 week goals, a keystone action, counted costs, and peers.
- 2018-01, Moran: "It creates an illusion that there is lots of time, which often results in procrastination, discouragement and failure." (S011)
- 2020-12, Moran: "Annual planning, annual goal setting is better than no goal setting. But there’s inherent barriers in it." (S022)
- 2022-07, Moran: "that's the difference between a true commitment and a New Year's resolution" (S047)

## Planning horizons
- **Stance:** Long-term vision (3, 5, 10 years) for direction; 12 weeks for goals and plans; the week for execution. In a fast-changing world, plans must be fluid.
- 2014-05, Moran: "Twelve weeks is long enough to make significant progress, but near enough that you don’t lose sight of the deadline." (S014)
- 2020-08, Moran: "we’ve moved from an environment where we need to be flexible to environment where we need to be fluid" (S023)
- 2020-12, Moran: "In fact, you can’t even plan accurately for 12 months." (S022)

## How many goals
- **Stance:** One is best, three is the ceiling; a business goal and a personal goal is a common pair.
- 2020-12, Moran: "it’s either you got a business goal or a personal goal" (S022)
- 2020-08, Moran: "we all have limited capacity, I don’t care how much energy you have, you have limited energy" (S023)

## Motivation versus discipline
- **Stance:** Do not wait to feel like it. Discipline is a daily choice that never becomes automatic for hard things, and it runs out, so back it with systems and peers.
- 2014-02, Moran: "There is no way around it... it takes discipline and rigor to be great." (S017)
- 2019-03, Moran: "The truth is that it continues to be a conscious choice that I have to make daily." (S003)
- 2022-07, Moran: "it's like a fuel tank. And every time you use it, it depletes the tank" (S047)
- **Tension:** 2019 Moran calls discipline "empowering, uplifting and freeing" (S003); 2022 Moran treats it as a depleting tank that systems must cover (S047). Newer view: lean on systems.

## Habits and routines
- **Stance:** Routines drive results; judge a routine by what it produces. Start and end the day on purpose. Lennington wrote the routine and systems posts.
- 2018-03, Lennington: "Routines can be either “good” or “bad”, the difference is the results that they create." (S008)
- 2018-10, Moran: "One of the best routines that I learned years ago was to start and end each day in gratitude." (S004)

## Health and training
- **Stance:** Moran uses his own training as the main example of execution: weights three days and cardio twice a week (2019), five workouts a week (2014). Showing up is the hard part; skipping a session saves little time and costs a lot.
- 2014-04, Moran: "the hardest part of the workout is showing up" (S015)
- 2019-03, Moran: "With diet and exercise the effort is significant and the benefits are delayed." (S003)
- 2020-12, Moran: weight loss as the model 12 week plan (download app, log food daily, lift four times a week) (S022).
- Moran studied physiology to be a strength coach (S023, S047).

## Sleep
- No recorded position.

## Stress and recovery
- **Stance:** Focus and knowing what matters most lowers stress. Plan time off every 13 weeks. Healthy urgency, not panic.
- 2020-12, Moran: "Which is one of the benefits of the 12-week year is it reduces your stress." (S022)
- 2020-08, Moran: "not stressed out urgency, like you might be in November, December" (S023)
- 2013-04, Moran: "Make a point of taking vacations... yes that’s plural, vacations." (S019)

## Focus and distraction
- **Stance:** The weekly plan is the filter. Email and voicemail come after. Most urgent things are not urgent.
- 2014-05, Moran: "Often the urgent is a great excuse not to work on the more important." (S014)
- 2020-12, Moran: "So, when 20 minutes opens up in my calendar for some reason, I’m not checking my e-mail" (S022)

## Social media and phones
- **Stance:** Shut the phone off for strategic blocks. Moran wants to shape his daughters' values more than their friends or Instagram do.
- 2014-05, Moran: "Carve out a time when you turn off your email, shut off your phone, and lock your door" (S014)
- 2013-04, Moran: "I want to be the one who shapes their values and their character, not their friends, not Instagram" (S019)

## Work hours and rest
- **Stance:** Not harder or longer; more consistent on the few things that matter. Quantity of work is not the point.
- 2020-08, Moran: "It’s not about working harder or longer. It’s really about just being more consistent with the things that matter" (S023)
- 2014-04, Moran: "dedicating more time is not always the best way to create a higher level of performance" (S015)

## Hustle and the hard-charger
- **Stance:** Effort is fine; scattered effort is the problem.
- 2020-08, Moran: "Did you really move the needle? Or are you the hamster on the wheel" (S023)

## Failure and setbacks
- **Stance:** Expect trials. Do not beat yourself up; learn, adjust, keep moving. A bad stretch lasts 12 weeks at most.
- 2013-03, Moran: "You will have trials. Be prepared. Learn and adapt." (S020)
- 2018-10, Moran: "Nothing good comes from chastising yourself or wallowing in self-pity and guilt." (S004)
- 2020-12, Moran: "you’re going to have weeks when you stumble. I have weeks when I stumble." (S022)

## Fear and risk
- **Stance:** Fear comes with growth and fades only by acting. Moran's favorite book is Feel the Fear and Do It Anyway, which he recommends in 2018, 2020, and 2022.
- 2018-05, Moran: "So the next time you are facing a goal, or action, that elicits some level of fear, be mindful of the 5 truths and Feel the Fear and Do It Anyway!" (S006)
- 2022-03, Moran: "anytime you’re going to do something that is uncomfortable for you, that seems a little outrageous, you know, there’s some discomfort that comes with that" (S043)

## Comfort
- **Stance:** Comfort is the main enemy of growth.
- 2020-12, Moran: "we are wired for comfort" (S022)
- 2018-02, Moran: "Are you intentional about your growth or your comfort?" (S009)

## Ambition versus contentment
- **Stance:** Ambitious: you are capable of more than you think. But the bigger payoff is who you become, and the vision is your whole life, not just work.
- 2020-08, Moran: "it shapes who you become in the process" (S023)
- 2018-02, Moran: "You get what you expect. If you expect little, you’ll get little." (S010)

## Comparison and other people's opinions
- **Stance:** A strong vision makes other people's opinions matter less. No position on envy recorded.
- 2020-12, Moran: "when you’ve got a strong vision, it lessens the impact of that, right? I don’t care so much about what other people think." (S022)

## Thinking and mindset
- **Stance:** Thinking drives action drives results; focus on solutions and gratitude, not problems.
- 2013-01, Moran: "Ultimately, your thinking creates your life." (S021)
- 2020-08, Moran: "you’re thinking create your action, structures, results" (S023)

## Faith
- **Stance:** Moran draws on the Bible in posts (trials, thinking on what is good) and closes with "Be encouraged".
- 2013-03, Moran: "Jesus said that you “will have trials”... not that you might, or you probably will, but that you will face trials." (S020)

## Relationships, marriage, and family
- **Stance:** Quantity time, not quality time. Define a family identity. Choose your spouse and kids over comfort in the moment.
- 2013-04, Moran: "In order to have influence I believe you need to spend time together... and not just “quality time,” but quantity time." (S019)
- 2020-08, Moran: "my kids know what it means to be a Moran" (S023)
- 2020-12, Moran: "Am I going to choose to be the father that has impact, that has influence or am I going to choose to sit here and watch the football game right now" (S022)

## Accountability partners
- **Stance:** Use peers, but not to "hold you accountable"; the burden is yours.
- 2020-12, Moran: "The burden to perform is yours." (S022)
- 2018-01, Moran: "If you decide to go it alone you are stacking the odds against yourself." (S011)

## Leading and coaching others
- **Stance:** Hold people capable, confront with choice, agree on specific actions. Command and control fails.
- 2022-07, Moran: "I don't like command and control." (S047)
- 2022-03, Lennington: "You can’t get all the way there if you apply negative consequences as your accountability model." (S043)

## Reading and learning
- **Stance:** Read for application. Lennington: read widely before writing.
- 2020-08, Moran: "knowledge for the sake of knowledge is great maybe in academia, but it’s not going to do much in my life" (S023)
- 2022-03, Lennington: "I’d read 10 books on the subject before you start writing it" (S043)

## Money and saving
- No recorded position on personal money. Only an analogy: Moran: "Most people use their time the way they save money" (S014).

## Legacy and character
- **Stance:** Keeping promises to yourself builds character; the person you become outlasts the result.
- 2017-11, Moran: "Keeping self promises builds character and esteem." (S012)

## Work-life balance
- **Stance:** Use the plan to live the life you want in every area, not sacrifice all for one. A third-party column (not kept) proposed cycling priorities by 12 weeks; the authors' own words here are more general.
- 2020-08, Moran: "it provides you the opportunity then to be to live the life you want in in all the areas of your life not just in one" (S023)

## Business views that shape how they live
- **Ideas:** Moran: "the marketplace only rewards the ideas that could implement it" (S023)
- **Leadership growth:** Moran: "the company isn’t going to outpace the leader" (S023)
- **His biggest mistake:** Moran: "I didn’t, I didn’t hire and develop leaders. And it really, it really held our company back." (S023)
