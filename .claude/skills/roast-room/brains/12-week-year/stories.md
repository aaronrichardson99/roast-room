# Stories: Brian Moran and Michael Lennington

Built from 29 public sources (2013 to 2022). 14 stories they reuse, with the lesson and source ids. Author named on each.

1. **Periodization in 12 weeks (both).** Needing something to hand out at a 2003 insurance conference, they wrote a short book in 12 weeks and printed a small run; it sold far beyond expectations and became The 12 Week Year. Lesson: you can do a lot in 12 weeks, and documenting what works beats theory. Copy counts differ by telling (200 given away, S018; 100 printed, "sold 100,000", S022; "a couple of hundred thousand", S043). Lennington adds the joke that they expected to store the copies in a garage, and that the "12 weeks" leaned on years of training material (S043).

2. **The investment advisor in COVID (Moran).** 7.2 million the prior year, 8.3 million in his last 12 weeks. Lesson: a near deadline creates focus. (S022)

3. **COVID broke the annual plan (Moran).** Everyone planned in January 2020, then everything changed. Lesson: annual plans cannot keep up; be fluid. (S022, S023)

4. **The weight-loss plan (Moran).** His model tactical plan: download an app in week 1, log food weeks 1 to 12, lift four times a week, cardio twice, hire a trainer if new. Lesson: tactics are discrete actions with due weeks. (S022)

5. **Running he does not enjoy (Moran).** He hates running; the hard part is showing up; once out, an extra five minutes is easy. Lesson: a little extra on a keystone action compounds. (S015)

6. **Four months of training and still a daily choice (Moran).** He hoped training would become automatic like brushing teeth; it did not. Lesson: discipline is a daily choice. (S003)

7. **The ten-speed bike (Moran).** At age 10 he worked for a 100 dollar bike by holding a picture of riding it. Lesson: strong desire carries commitment. (S012)

8. **Sam Lowell quit (Moran).** A coach asks if Jordan quit, if the Wright brothers quit; "Who's Sam Lowell?" "Of course you've never heard of him, he quit!" Lesson: persistence. (S007)

9. **Lost everything in 2008 (Moran).** The recession wiped them out and they re-engineered the company fast. (S023)

10. **Everything ran through me (Moran).** His biggest mistake was not hiring and developing leaders, while coaching others to do it. Lesson: grow the leader first. (S023)

11. **UPS reload supervisor (Moran).** A coaching boss in a union shop switched him on to business; he dropped strength coaching. (S023, S047)

12. **Learning to walk (Moran).** If toddlers feared embarrassment like adults, they would never walk. Lesson: a vision lets you risk falling. (S022)

13. **Quantity time with his girls (Moran).** Orlando spring breaks, raising and showing Arabian horses, an inside joke about yelling "shark". Lesson: influence takes hours, not minutes. (S019, S022)

14. **Lennington's Delta COVID deadline (Lennington).** Sick near the Uncommon Accountability deadline, he wanted to push it back, then admitted he had procrastinated earlier. Lesson: own it and move forward. (S043)

15. **April 15 (Moran).** Pay your taxes or go to prison: you dislike both, but you still have choice. Lesson: you always have choice. (S047)
