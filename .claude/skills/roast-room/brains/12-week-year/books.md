# Books: Brian Moran and Michael Lennington

Built from what the authors say about their books in 29 public sources. No book text is used. Theses and frameworks are in our own words.

## Periodization: 12 Weeks to Breakthrough (self-published, about 2003)
- **Thesis:** Borrow periodization from athletic training and apply it to business: short cycles, focused goals, and execution disciplines.
- **Story:** Moran says they started it in late November 2002 for an insurance conference (LAMP) in March and wrote it in 12 weeks (S018). Copy counts vary by telling: 200 printed and all given away (S018); 100 printed at Kinko's and "sold 100,000 copies" (S022); 100 sold at 10 dollars, then "a couple of hundred thousand" (S043). Lennington says 99.9 percent of their leads came from it (S043).
- **Taught in:** S018, S022, S043, S047.

## The 12 Week Year (Wiley, 2013)
- **Thesis:** Annual planning hides the deadline. Treat each 12 weeks as a year, set one to three goals tied to a life vision, write a tactical plan, run it weekly, score execution weekly, and meet peers weekly. Expanded from Periodization with an application section (S018).
- **Key frameworks:** 12 weeks as the year; five disciplines (vision, planning, process control, measurement, time use); the tactical 12 week plan; the weekly plan and scorecard with an 85 percent line; strategic time blocks; peer accountability; accountability, commitment, and intentionality chapters (Moran cites "the chapter on Intentionality", S009).
- **Taught in:** S002, S009, S011, S014, S018, S022, S023, S047.
- **Where the authors differ on its origin:** Lennington says they created it "out of whole cloth" (S043); Moran says it documents what already worked with clients (S043, S022, S047).

## The 12 Week Year Field Guide (Wiley, 2016)
- **Thesis:** The workbook for the method: vision exercises, building the 12 week plan, and templates (described on their site, S030).
- **Taught in:** S030 (site description only). No interview in the corpus covers it, so the brain has no detail beyond the 12 Week Year material.

## The 12 Week Year for Writers (Trevor Thrall, 2021)
- **Thesis:** The method applied to writing.
- **Authorship conflict:** Lennington says it "wasn't written by Brian and I" (S043); the Uncommon Accountability site lists it as one of Lennington's co-authored books (S046). Treat it as Thrall's book built on theirs.

## Uncommon Accountability (Wiley, 2022)
- **Thesis:** Accountability is ownership of free choice, not consequences. Leaders should stop "holding people accountable" with escalating punishment and "hold them capable" by confronting them with their choices and the consequences of each.
- **Key frameworks:** accountability as ownership; the victim mindset as rear-view mirror; hold capable and confront with choice; agreement at the goal and action levels; clarity, transparency, evidence; productive tension in peer groups.
- **Taught in:** S043, S046, S047, S052. Moran says it grew from the accountability chapters in The 12 Week Year (S047); Lennington calls accountability the foundational principle of the 12 Week Year "20 years ago" (S043).
