# Principles: Brian Moran and Michael Lennington

Built from 29 public sources (2013 to 2022): blog posts, long interviews, and site copy. 20 decision rules. Each quote names its author. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Execution, not knowledge, is the gap.** Moran: "the breakdown is never not knowing, it’s not doing" (S022) Moran: "Knowledge is only power if you act on it." (S014) Sources: 6 (S001, S014, S022, S023, S030, S049). Confidence: high.

2. **Treat 12 weeks as the year; annual plans hide the deadline.** Moran: "In January, December looks a long way off" (S014) Moran: "Forget, annual goal setting forget annual planning, it’s just you’ll do better with it than none at all, but it’s really ineffective." (S023) Sources: 5 (S011, S014, S022, S023, S047). Confidence: high.

3. **You control actions, not outcomes, so measure actions.** Moran: "you don’t control the outcomes. You control your actions." (S022) Moran: "We control our actions, not their outcomes." (S014) Sources: 5 (S004, S014, S022, S047, S002). Confidence: high.

4. **Concepts do not execute; write actions.** Moran: "Concepts don’t execute, you’ve got to get tactical." (S022) Moran: "no one is going to sign up and take ownership of something that's vague" (S047) Sources: 4 (S014, S022, S023, S047). Confidence: high.

5. **Less is more: one goal beats three.** Moran: "One’s better than two, two’s better than three. When you start to get over three, you’re stuck the odds against yourself." (S022) Moran: "Most plans have too much in them." (S014) Sources: 4 (S011, S014, S022, S023). Confidence: high.

6. **Start from a life vision, and stay connected to it.** Moran: "We work with life vision because business is just part of life" (S014) Moran: "the vision creates the passion, the passion creates the courage to do the things you need to do" (S022) Sources: 6 (S004, S011, S014, S020, S022, S023). Confidence: high.

7. **Consistency beats perfection; 85 percent is the line.** Unsigned blog: "You don’t need to be perfect, just consistent and persistent." (S002) Moran: "you don’t have to be perfect with the execution anyways" (S023) Moran: "the thought that we're going to be perfect with that, I think is just setting yourself up to fail" (S047) Sources: 4 (S002, S022, S023, S047). Confidence: high.

8. **Win this week; do not try to catch up.** Moran: "I win this week. I don’t worry about next week till next week." (S022) Sources: 2 (S022, S014). Confidence: medium.

9. **Act on commitments, not feelings.** Moran: "Learning to do the things you know you need to do regardless of how you feel is a core discipline for success." (S012) Moran: "You can’t wait until you feel like engaging with your goals." (S004) Sources: 4 (S003, S004, S012, S047). Confidence: high.

10. **Count the cost before the moment of choice.** Moran: "if I have to decide it in the moment, in my warm bed, with my head on the pillow, the odds are against me getting out and getting after it" (S047) Sources: 3 (S011, S012, S047). Confidence: high.

11. **Sacrifice comfort, never your values or health.** Moran: "you don’t have to sacrifice your values, you don’t have to sacrifice your integrity, your health, your sanity, your family" (S022) Moran: "But you got to sacrifice your comfort, because if you’re going to grow, if you’re going to do anything great, it’s going to be uncomfortable" (S022) Sources: 4 (S011, S017, S022, S009). Confidence: high.

12. **Confidence follows action and reps.** Moran: "You can’t sit here and think your way into feeling confident. It only comes through actions." (S022) Moran: "the “doing it” comes before the feeling better about yourself" (S006) Sources: 3 (S006, S020, S022). Confidence: high.

13. **No guilt: look at last week candidly, learn, move on.** Moran: "There’s a lot of grace in the 12-week year, because you can’t change anything you did last week." (S022) Moran: "we don’t deal in guilt" (S043) Sources: 5 (S004, S011, S022, S043, S047). Confidence: high.

14. **Accountability is ownership; you always have a choice.** Moran: "Accountability is ownership. It’s the recognition that we have free will choice." (S022) Moran: "we always, always always have choice" (S047) Sources: 4 (S022, S043, S046, S047). Confidence: high.

15. **Do not go alone; use peers and productive tension.** Moran: "we will let ourselves down easier than we will if someone's looking in someone else's watching" (S047) Sources: 3 (S011, S022, S047). Confidence: high.

16. **Lean on systems, because discipline runs out.** Lennington: "Every system that you build frees time." (S005) Moran: "it's like a fuel tank. And every time you use it, it depletes the tank" (S047) Sources: 3 (S005, S008, S047). Confidence: high.

17. **Check execution before you change the plan.** Moran: "65 percent of the time, the problem is the execution" (S014) Moran: "most of the breakdowns aren't in the plan, most of the breakdowns are at the execution level" (S047) Sources: 2 (S014, S047). Confidence: medium.

18. **There is no perfect plan; adjust in small steps.** Moran: "Every plan you ever write as flawed. So, the thing you got to do is go out in the marketplace and succeed or fail as fast as you can." (S022) Moran: "Some action is always better than no action." (S020) Sources: 3 (S020, S022, S023). Confidence: high.

19. **Protect strategic time first; the urgent is often an excuse.** Moran: "Most of the urgent stuff isn’t as urgent as we make it" (S014) Moran: "If the day’s controlling me, I have no power over my destiny." (S014) Lennington: "it’s being realistic about the time you have available and blocking out the time to write and then writing in those time blocks" (S043) Sources: 3 (S009, S014, S043). Confidence: high.

20. **Celebrate every cycle.** Moran: "What you celebrate matters. It creates focus, it creates culture, it creates momentum." (S022) Sources: 2 (S013, S022). Confidence: medium.
