# Principles: James Clear

Built from 75 sources (2012 to 2026): articles, annual reviews, integrity reports, 3-2-1 newsletters, and long interviews. 26 decision rules. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Fix the system, not the result.** Results are a lagging measure of habits. "Fix the inputs and the outputs will fix themselves." (S010) "almost all the results that you want are a lagging measure of your habits." (S003) Sources: 7 (S003, S004, S005, S010, S029, S074, S075). Confidence: high.

2. **Start with who you want to become, then prove it with small wins.** "Decide the type of person you want to be. Prove it to yourself with small wins." (S072) "New identities require new evidence." (S018) Sources: 9 (S001, S004, S005, S006, S018, S070, S072, S074, S076). Confidence: high.

3. **Make it so small you can do it on your worst day.** "It’s far better to do less than you hoped than to do nothing at all." (S013) "Make your habits so easy that you can stick to them even on the hard days." (S022) "instead of asking ourselves, what could I do on my best day? It's better to start by asking, what can I stick to even on the bad days." (S004) Sources: 8 (S004, S013, S022, S027, S029, S046, S074, S076). Confidence: high.

4. **Master the art of showing up before you optimize.** "The point is to master the habit of showing up." (S013) "You need to standardize before you optimize." (S076) Sources: 6 (S003, S004, S013, S046, S074, S076). Confidence: high.

5. **Change the environment instead of summoning willpower.** "What often looks like a lack of willpower is actually the result of a poor environment." (S014) "If your environment doesn’t change, you probably won’t either." (S029) Sources: 10 (S003, S014, S015, S017, S029, S032, S047, S071, S074, S076). Confidence: high.

6. **Never miss twice; the comeback matters more than the slip.** "the first mistake is never the one that ruins you. It is the spiral of repeated mistakes that follows." (S022) "what separates top performers from everyone else is that they get back on track very quickly." (S017) Sources: 9 (S004, S008, S011, S017, S022, S023, S051, S074, S076). Confidence: high.

7. **Do not throw up a zero; reduce the scope and keep the schedule.** "if you don't throw up a zero, you maintain the habit." (S004) "Don’t have enough time to do a full workout? Just squat." (S023) Sources: 6 (S003, S004, S006, S007, S008, S023). Confidence: high.

8. **Consistency beats intensity.** "people need consistency more than they need intensity." (S004) "Consistency enlarges ability." (S004) Sources: 4 (S004, S006, S040, S062). Confidence: high.

9. **Motivation follows action, so do not wait for it.** "motivation often comes after starting." (S034) "You have to show up before inspiration will." (S069) "Motivation is short lived and doesn’t lead to consistent action." (S020) Sources: 7 (S004, S008, S020, S024, S034, S069, S035). Confidence: high.

10. **Set a schedule to operate by, not a deadline to perform by.** "give yourself a schedule to follow, not a deadline to race toward." (S035) "Professionals stick to the schedule, amateurs let life get in the way." (S024) Sources: 4 (S024, S035, S040, S065). Confidence: high.

11. **Choose the version you enjoy; fun is a strategy.** "what would it look like if this was fun? What would it look like if your habits were fun?" (S004) "Choose the version of a habit that best suits you, not the one that is most popular." (S061) Sources: 6 (S003, S004, S006, S061, S075, S076). Confidence: high.

12. **Surround yourself with people who already live the way you want to live.** "Surround yourself with people who live the way you want to live." (S017) "Join groups where your desired behavior is normal." (S004) Sources: 6 (S003, S004, S017, S050, S074, S076). Confidence: high.

13. **Decide how you want to spend your days before chasing the result.** "to want the outcome without the lifestyle is to like torture yourself." (S004) "why make a trade to live a worse life just so that you can have more status or more money?" (S075) Sources: 5 (S003, S004, S006, S007, S075). Confidence: high.

14. **Every yes is a no to everything else; guard your time.** "When you say no, you are only saying no to one option. When you say yes, you are saying no to every other option." (S037) "success generates not only opportunities, but also distractions." (S003) Sources: 7 (S003, S007, S036, S037, S062, S063, S070). Confidence: high.

15. **Eliminate before you optimize.** "elimination is a more useful skill than optimization." (S037) "Prune away the inessential. Pour yourself into what remains." (S063) Sources: 6 (S036, S037, S038, S039, S059, S063). Confidence: high.

16. **Accept that every choice has a cost, then be at peace with the trade-off.** "Every choice has a cost." (S033) "What's a natural recipe for disaster is to make that choice, but then to not be okay with the trade-off." (S075) Sources: 4 (S033, S063, S004, S075). Confidence: high.

17. **Watch the trajectory, not the position.** "You should be far more concerned with your current trajectory than with your current results." (S055) "The trajectory of your life bends in the direction of your habits." (S003) Sources: 5 (S003, S004, S055, S062, S075). Confidence: high.

18. **Be patient: results are delayed and the work is stored.** "the work isn't wasted. It's just being stored." (S005) "Mastery requires both impatience and patience." (S062) Sources: 5 (S005, S041, S061, S062, S073). Confidence: high.

19. **Recovery is part of the plan.** "Recovery is non-negotiable. The bill always comes due. Push yourself hard and you’ll have to take it easy at some point." (S061) "there is no substitute for sleeping." (S045) Sources: 3 (S045, S059, S061). Confidence: high.

20. **Prefer flexible over rigid; needing perfect conditions makes you brittle.** "if you need things to be a certain way, then you're being held hostage by the situation." (S006) "Being consistent is not the same as being perfect." (S051) Sources: 5 (S004, S006, S007, S051, S028). Confidence: high.

21. **Compare narrowly or not at all.** "comparison is like the teacher of skills when it's applied narrowly, but it's the thief of joy when it's applied broadly." (S004) "competitiveness does not necessarily require you to compare." (S008) Sources: 4 (S004, S007, S008, S055). Confidence: high.

22. **Start before you feel ready.** "successful people start before they feel ready." (S042) "Stop worrying about how long it will take and get started. Time will pass either way." (S067) Sources: 3 (S042, S067, S069). Confidence: high.

23. **Put in the reps; volume teaches more than planning.** "Everyone wants to make progress. And there is only one way to do it: put in your reps." (S026) "A lot of people put in 10,000 hours. Very few people put in 10,000 hours of revision." (S049) Sources: 3 (S026, S049, S004). Confidence: high.

24. **Live like the person you claim to be, and make it right when you slip.** "Am I actually living like the type of person I claim to be?" (S065) "I won’t be perfect in this journey, but when I do screw up I will make things right." (S065) Sources: 3 (S064, S065, S062). Confidence: high.

25. **Be kind first, be right later.** "Be kind first, be right later." (S056) "it doesn't really cost you very much to be kind." (S074) Sources: 2 (S056, S074). Confidence: medium.

26. **Reflect and review on a schedule.** "reflection and review is a really critical part of living a good life." (S006) "If you never decide on a vision for your life, you’ll often find yourself living someone else’s dream." (S044) Sources: 6 (S003, S004, S006, S044, S057, S075). Confidence: high.
