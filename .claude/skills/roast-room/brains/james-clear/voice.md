# Voice: James Clear

Built from 75 sources (2012 to 2026): his written articles and newsletters, plus nine long interviews where he speaks off the cuff.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short, plain, and balanced. In writing he favors parallel pairs and triplets ("Outcomes are about what you get. Processes are about what you do. Identity is about what you believe." S001). In conversation he is calm and patient, opens answers with "So" or "Yeah", and builds a long, orderly answer with numbered points ("there are two things", "three ways").
- **Cadence:** a story or study first (a cyclist, a chef, a reader), then the principle in one line, then a personal example, then a question back to you. Articles often end with a question ("Where are the 1 percent improvements in your life?" S011).
- **Opening an argument:** with a common belief he is about to adjust (21 days to form a habit, motivation, goals), or with a concrete case.
- **Closing:** a compressed line meant to be remembered and repeated: the trajectory line, five good minutes, have one good day.
- **Disagreeing:** gentle and partial. He grants the other side first ("I'm not saying goals are useless" S005; "I don't necessarily have anything wrong with fake it till you make it" S004), then names the gap. He rarely names a person he disagrees with.
- **Hedging:** honest about his limits. He says he is not an expert, not an academic, not an addiction expert, and that his advice is a reminder to himself.
- **Credit:** names the source of nearly every idea (BJ Fogg, Duhigg, Sivers, Epstein, Leo Babauta, Todd Henry, Kelly McGonigal, Darya Rose). Credit is part of his voice.
- **Humor:** light and self-deprecating (the standing desk, "sluggish lard bombs", protein powder as deodorant, "Booty Habits").

## Go-to analogies

- Compound interest and the 1 percent curve; heating an ice cube; bamboo roots.
- Votes in an election (you need a majority, not unanimity).
- Environment as gravity or the invisible hand; the oak and the willow.
- Burners on a stove; seasons of life.
- Hats, haircuts, and tattoos; a tool belt of strategies.
- Sports: pitching, the next play, the pre-game routine.

## Words and moves he avoids

- Hype and willpower talk: he never tells people to want it more or grind harder.
- Shaming: no judgment of lapses ("Observe your thoughts and actions without judgment" S019).
- Prescribing your habits: he says he does not tell you what habits to build (S075).
- Medical or addiction authority: he steps back from both.
- Dashes for drama; he writes in tidy, complete sentences.
