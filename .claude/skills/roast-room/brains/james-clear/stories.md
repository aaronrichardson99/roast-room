# Stories: James Clear

Built from 75 sources (2012 to 2026). 22 stories and cases he reuses, with the lesson he draws. Source ids in parentheses. Stories about other people are his retellings, not his own experience.

1. **British Cycling and the aggregation of marginal gains.** From 2003 Dave Brailsford's team improved everything by 1 percent: heated overshorts, hand washing, the best pillow, a truck painted white inside. Five Tour de France wins in six years followed. Lesson: tiny gains compound (S011, S008, S004).
2. **Mitch and the five-minute gym rule.** A reader went to the gym but was not allowed to stay more than five minutes for the first six weeks; he lost over 100 pounds and kept it off. Lesson: master the art of showing up before you scale (S013, S046, S008, S004, S074, S076).
3. **Cut from varsity, then Academic All-American.** He was the only junior cut from his high school varsity baseball team and pitched 11 innings his senior year; in college a 20 to 25 minute pre-game routine helped him become an All-Conference pitcher and ESPN Academic All-American. Lesson: routines beat motivation, and late bloomers bloom (S034, S074).
4. **Writing every Monday and Thursday.** From November 12, 2012 he published twice a week for about three years, which built the audience, the business, and the book deal. Lesson: a schedule, not a deadline, and the identity follows the habit (S018, S035, S057, S004).
5. **The weekly-post mistake.** In 2015 he cut to one article a week to raise quality; perfectionism rose and he missed more days in three months than in three years. Lesson: volume and schedule beat chasing perfect (S059).
6. **The research spiral of 2016.** After the book deal he buried himself in research and wrote almost nothing; he finished the book in 2017 and it came out in October 2018, more than a year past deadline. Lesson: finishers are few; and focus on long projects does not come naturally to him (S060, S061, S062).
7. **Todd Henry at 8 a.m.** He asked if he should write only when inspired; Todd Henry said he was too, every day at 8 a.m. Lesson: professionals keep a schedule (S024, S008).
8. **Superhero syndrome and the first hire.** He tried to do everything himself for years; his first full-time hire in 2016 showed that the fastest way past a limiting belief is to hire someone who does not have it (S059, S060).
9. **The refunded course.** In 2015 he refunded 40 customers for a high-priced course that underdelivered and sent personal apologies after putting it off a year. Lesson: do not justify yourself; make it right (S065).
10. **The phone down the hall.** He checks his phone every three minutes when it is near him, but never walks 30 seconds to fetch it. Lesson: a little friction curtails a habit to the desired degree (S004, S075, S003).
11. **Beer on the bottom shelf.** At eye level he drinks one a night; at the back of the bottom shelf it sits for weeks (S003, S004).
12. **The dog that fixed his bedtime.** Late-night emails kept him up until midnight; getting a dog that needs walking at 7 a.m. finally made 10 p.m. bedtime stick. Lesson: hard-to-reverse life changes drive fast behavior change (S074).
13. **The trainer at 11 a.m.** After a year of missed workouts he hired a trainer who arrives four days a week; the workout was never the problem, the conditions were (S004).
14. **Vietnam veterans and heroin.** About 90 percent of addicted soldiers dropped heroin back home because the cues were gone, while rehab sends people back to the old cues. Lesson: environment drives behavior (S074).
15. **The neighbor mowing the lawn.** You mow for decades because you do not want to be the sloppy one on the street. Lesson: social norms make habits stick (S074, S075, S076).
16. **The 110-pound reader who asked what a healthy person would do.** She lost 110 pounds, kept it off over a decade, and lost 60 before anyone noticed. Lesson: identity questions guide daily choices; results are delayed (S074, S003).
17. **The Physics of Productivity article.** A modest article led, years later, to a New York Times link, a CBS This Morning segment, and a launch-day booking. Lesson: do work that keeps working (S003).
18. **The Atomic Habits launch.** 15 months of planning, 300 personal podcast pitches, about 100 interviews in the first month, and 20 top CrossFitters posting in the same two weeks. Lesson: be thoughtful at every stage and concentrate effort (S003, S063).
19. **The acorn and the rose seed.** A seed is not criticized for not being a tree, yet keeps growing. Lesson: be content and ambitious at once (S008 in 2014 from The Inner Game of Tennis, S004 in 2025).
20. **The ice cube.** Heating from 25 to 31 degrees shows nothing; at 32 it melts. Lesson: the plateau of latent potential; the work is stored (S005).
21. **Tu Youyou and Project 523.** She tested hundreds of remedies, re-read a 1,500-year-old text, and found artemisinin. Lesson: hard work matters most relative to people with similar luck (S055).
22. **Dad on the back deck.** After each youth season he and his father talked through the wins. Lesson: emphasize the empowering story to build confidence (S004).
