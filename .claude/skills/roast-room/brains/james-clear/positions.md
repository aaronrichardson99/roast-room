# Positions: James Clear

Built from 75 sources (2012 to 2026). 32 topics, written for a personal board: habits, health, time, money, relationships, and how work shapes a life. Each stance has a verbatim quote, source id, and date (article date, or recording date for interviews; see `sources.md`). Where his view shifted, the versions sit side by side with dates, newest marked. Topics with no evidence say so.

## Habits and routines (how to start one)
- **Stance:** Start tiny, tie it to a cue, make it obvious and easy, and repeat until it is part of who you are. One new habit at a time.
- "When you start a new habit, it should take less than two minutes to do." (S013, 2013-05)
- "Personally, I prefer to focus on building one new behavior into my life at a time." (S029, 2015-01)
- 2024: "Your habits are often a byproduct of convenience." (S071, 2025-01)

## How long a habit takes to form
- **Stance:** No magic number. Lally's study averaged 66 days with a range of 18 to 254 (S028, 2014-03). His real answer: "How long does it take? The honest answer is: forever. Because once you stop doing it, it is no longer a habit." (S022, 2019-01) Repeated in 2025: "My answer is forever." (S004, 2025-12)

## Motivation versus discipline
- **Stance:** Neither is the engine. Motivation is fickle and comes after you start; discipline in practice means a schedule, an easy start, and a good environment.
- 2013: "If you want consistent change the last thing you want to rely on is something inconsistent." (S023, 2013-03)
- 2014: "motivation comes after starting, not before." (S008, 2014-12)
- 2024: "motivation rises and falls. So if you happen to have it, capitalize on it" (S006, 2024-12)
- The people who look disciplined are tempted less: "in the long run, your willpower will almost never beat your environment." (S076, 2024-01)

## Mental toughness and grit
- **Shift over time:**
  - 2013: grit is the top predictor and "In other words, talent is overrated." (S040, 2013-04) "In a word, toughness and grit equal consistency." (S040, 2013-04)
  - 2018: "You are only as mentally tough as your life demands you to be." (S062, 2018-12) "When life doesn’t challenge you, challenge yourself." (S062, 2018-12)
  - 2021 to 2025 (newer): toughness is flexibility and handling uncertainty, and genes shape where grit shows up (grit is fit, a line from David Epstein; S004, S074). "the ultimate form of mental toughness is the ability to handle uncertainty." (S075, 2024-08) "if you need things to be a certain way, then you're being held hostage by the situation." (S006, 2024-12)
- **Read:** he moved from grind and be consistent toward bend, do the small version, and find work that fits you (inferred from S040, S004, S006, S074).

## Goal setting
- **Stance:** Goals set direction; systems make progress. Set the goal, then shelve it.
- 2013: "Goals are good for setting a direction, but systems are best for making progress." (S010, 2013-10)
- 2018: "I'm not saying goals are useless. I think goals still have a purpose. And the purpose is that they provide clarity." (S005, 2018-10)
- 2025: "goals are best for people who care about winning once. Systems are best for people who care about winning repeatedly." (S004, 2025-12)
- On proxies: "Don’t let a proxy become the target. Don’t optimize for the wrong outcome." (S067, 2020-07)

## New Year's resolutions
- **Stance:** Fine to use the fresh-start energy, but start from identity, plan for the slip, and do not wait for January.
- "rather than starting by asking yourself, what do I wish to achieve? Let's start by asking who do I wish to become?" (S006, 2024-12)
- Pre-mortem: "it's so unlikely that you will stick to a habit." (S076, 2024-01) forever without interruption, so plan the comeback.

## Planning horizons
- **Stance:** Ten years and the next hour. Think in decades of meaningful work, act today.
- "the two timeframes that matter most in life are 10 years and one hour." (S004, 2025-12)
- "Long-term thinking changes your short-term actions." (S073, 2026-10)
- Seasons and sequencing: "life has a series of seasons and life has a series of sequences." (S004, 2025-12)

## Focus and distraction
- **Stance:** Fewer priorities, single-tasking, and ruthless elimination.
- "Do the most important thing first each day. It’s the only productivity trick you need." (S039, 2015-07)
- "The most dangerous distractions are the ones you love, but that don’t love you back." (S036, 2014-10)
- "You’re not focused enough unless you’re mourning some of the things you’re saying no to." (S070, 2024-01)

## Phones and social media
- **Stance:** Keep the phone out of reach; he does not trust himself near it. Use social media for ideas, never your face, and own your audience by email.
- 2015: deleted all apps for a month: "I missed them very little." (S029, 2015-01)
- 2023 to 2025: "If my phone is right next to me, I will check it every three minutes just because it's there." (S004, 2025-12) Phone in another room until lunch most days (S003, S075).
- Admits overusing Twitter: "probably spending more time on it than I should" (S003, 2023-01).
- "I want the ideas to be front and center." (S076, 2024-01)

## News and information diet
- **Stance:** Skip the news; curate inputs because your thoughts are downstream of them.
- 2014: "I tend to not watch any news or television really, unless it's an Ohio State football game." (S008, 2014-12)
- 2023: "The person who creates better information flows, gets better thoughts." (S003, 2023-01)

## Reading and learning
- **Stance:** Read daily, quit books fast, read for the useful sentence.
- 2014: "20 pages per day. That’s all you need." (S054, 2014-07)
- 2023: "start more books, quit most of them." (S003, 2023-01)
- 2024: "I don't even really read books anymore. I kind of like read chapters or read sentences." (S076, 2024-01)
- "Curiosity is crucial." (S062, 2018-12)

## Sleep
- **Stance:** Non-negotiable. Eight hours; he averages eight to nine.
- "Most adults should be aiming for eight hours per night." (S045, 2014-07)
- "one of my cardinal rules is that I never cheat myself on sleep (I average 8 to 9 hours per night)" (S059, 2016-01)
- Working late to get more done backfires: "the drop in performance ruins any potential benefits of working additional hours." (S045, 2014-07)

## Stress, recovery, and burnout
- **Stance:** Recovery is part of performance; bursts end in crashes. Bad habits are usually a way to cope with stress and boredom, so replace them.
- "Recovery is non-negotiable. The bill always comes due." (S061, 2017-12)
- "Most of the time, bad habits are simply a way of dealing with stress and boredom." (S017, 2013-05)

## Exercise and training
- **Stance:** His keystone habit. Never miss a workout, keep it simple, lift weights; the session can shrink but should not vanish.
- 2012: "fitness is the keystone habit the puts the rest of my life in place." (S030, 2012-12)
- 2014: "in the first 6 months, it is more important to not miss workouts than it is to make progress." (S046, 2014-09)
- 2018: fundamentals list includes "lift weights 3x week" (S062, 2018-12).
- 2024: "my workout habit has rescued me a lot" (S075, 2024-08). He built a home gym (S003) and hired a trainer after a year of missed workouts (S004, 2025-12).
- Limits: counts macros; exercise always came easier to him than nutrition (S074, 2021-11).

## Nutrition and diet
- **Stance:** Design the kitchen, not the willpower; average over a week; aim for mostly, not perfectly.
- "Is eating clean five days per week better than not eating clean at all? Yes, I believe it is." (S023, 2013-03)
- "Josh doesn’t care what you eat for any individual meal." (S051, 2014-04)
- Admits: "like most people, I probably think I eat better than I actually do." (S059, 2016-01)
- Ate his first meal around noon (intermittent fasting) in 2013 (S053, 2013-06). No recorded current view on fasting.

## Alcohol and other vices
- **Stance:** Use friction and context; addiction is outside his lane.
- Beer on the bottom shelf sits for weeks (S003, 2023-01; S004, 2025-12).
- "I don't think this would work if you like, you know, actually have an addiction" (S004, 2025-12) "I don't claim to be an expert on addiction." (S075, 2024-08)
- Drinking less around heavy drinkers is "just playing the game on hard mode" (S076, 2024-01)

## Money and saving
- **Stance:** Money follows habits; save automatically; once you have enough, optimize for time and mission, not more money.
- "save at least 10 percent of your income" (S062, 2018-12)
- "your bank account is a lagging measure of your financial habits." (S003, 2023-01)
- 2014: "money and wealth is the only addiction that we applaud." (S008, 2014-12)
- 2018: "I want to optimize for reach over revenue. I don’t care about making the most money." (S062, 2018-12)
- Gives 5 percent of after-tax profits to the Against Malaria Foundation (S062, 2018-12).

## Investing and financial risk
- **Stance:** Thin record. Build wealth, not market-beating: "The real goal is not to “beat the market.” The goal is to build wealth." (S067, 2020-07) "When you intend to invest long-term, it changes which stocks you buy." (S073, 2026-10) No recorded position on debt, leverage, or asset allocation.

## Risk and starting things
- **Stance:** Start before you feel ready; test cheaply; most decisions are reversible.
- "If you’re working on something important, then you’ll never feel ready." (S042, 2012-11)
- "most decisions in life are hats and haircuts." (S004, 2025-12)
- His own history: fearful when young, "there were areas that I avoided because I thought I would struggle." (S074, 2021-11)

## Ambition versus contentment
- **Stance:** Not opposites. Be content at each stage and keep growing because that is what you do.
- 2014: "I'm also never going to stop growing simply because that's what I do." (S008, 2014-12) Admitted then: "this is something I certainly haven't mastered to any degree." (S008, 2014-12)
- 2025 (newer): "I can both be driven and be satisfied." (S004, 2025-12)
- Recalibrate: "you need to remind yourself when you wanted what you currently have." (S007, 2021-01)

## Happiness and meaning
- **Stance:** Happiness is a practice of returning to contentment; meaningful work is not always happy work.
- "you can practice returning to a state of contentment or returning to a state of not wanting." (S005, 2018-10)
- "I don't think your habits will lead you to someplace where you are just permanently happy." (S004, 2025-12)
- Goals delay happiness: "When you fall in love with the process rather than the product, you don’t have to wait to give yourself permission to be happy." (S010, 2013-10)

## Failure and setbacks
- **Stance:** A slip is a data point; quit tactics and strategies freely, a vision rarely.
- "These small hiccups don’t make you a failure, they make you human." (S023, 2013-03)
- "telling someone to never give up is terrible advice. Successful people give up all the time." (S044, 2016-05)
- "the secret to winning is knowing how to lose." (S004, 2025-12)

## Perfectionism
- **Stance:** Perfectionism is his own recurring failure mode; quantity and schedule beat it.
- "It’s not the quest to achieve one perfect goal that makes you better, it’s the skills you develop from doing a volume of work." (S026, 2014-01)
- His 2015 switch to weekly posts to raise quality: "That’s when I did something stupid: I changed the whole damn way I wrote." (S059, 2016-01)

## Ego, credit, and humility
- **Stance:** Credits others constantly; says he is a synthesizer, not an originator.
- "almost nothing that I share is something that I originally thought of." (S076, 2024-01)
- "nearly everything I write about, I have learned from someone else." (S065, 2016-06)
- Wants "a brand that's known by name, not by face." (S076, 2024-01)

## Comparison and envy
- **Stance:** Compare tactics, not lives; compare with people of similar luck; focus on your own slope.
- "comparison is like the teacher of skills when it's applied narrowly, but it's the thief of joy when it's applied broadly." (S004, 2025-12)
- "When you compare yourself to those who have experienced similar levels of luck, the difference is in your habits and choices." (S055, 2018-09)
- "keep your eyes on your own paper." (S057, 2013-12)

## Relationships and family
- **Stance:** People shape everything downstream; schedule the relationships that matter; be present when you are there.
- 2013 weakness: "Making people wait on me. I know I do it too often, especially with family and friends." (S057, 2013-12)
- 2025: "Relationships in general are probably, it's obvious to everybody that they matter, and yet they still are probably perpetually undervalued." (S004, 2025-12) "opportunities come through people." (S004, 2025-12)
- Marriage: the most important decision you will probably make (S004, 2025-12). He and his wife pull each other to the gym (S074, 2021-11).
- Small daily acts: "Five good minutes of conversation can restore a relationship." (S003, 2023-01)

## Friends and cutting people off
- **Stance:** Find places where the habit thrives instead of firing friends; distance only from the truly toxic.
- "sometimes you hear people say things like fire your friends, you know, or whatever. Like, and I, I'm not, I'm not all the way there." (S004, 2025-12)
- Change minds through friendship: "Facts don’t change our minds. Friendship does." (S056, 2018-09)

## Parenting and work hours
- **Shift over time:**
  - 2016: five years in an entrepreneurship season: "I turned my friends burner way down and my family burner is only running halfway." (S033, 2016-05)
  - 2021: as a new father, took three months off; now "two sacred hours every morning" for writing (S007, 2021-01).
  - 2024: about "40% of the working hours that I had before" and took 18 months to two years to adjust (S075, 2024-08).
  - 2025 (newest): "right now when my kids are young like i'm i'm going to turn the career burner down and that's fine" (S004, 2025-12)

## Work hours and rest
- **Stance:** Being a pro is not being a workaholic; protect a few deep hours, then stop.
- "Becoming a pro doesn’t mean you’re a workaholic." (S024, 2013-01)
- Hard work becomes a crutch: "if you just keep your head down and work hard, it's very unlikely that you'll be spending your time in the highest and best way." (S004, 2025-12)
- Optimistic view of time leaves no margin: "I’ve been told that I have a very optimistic view of time" (S062, 2018-12)

## Saying yes to opportunities
- **Stance:** Upgrade your no as you succeed; he admits he is still bad at it.
- "I seem to be very dumb and slow at learning it. Like I keep saying yes to things that I should not be saying yes to." (S007, 2021-01)
- "A life well-lived is a life well-edited." (S063, 2020-01)

## Travel
- **Stance:** Loves it, but heavy travel wrecks habits; schedule trips that matter and have an away-court routine.
- "When something is important, but not urgent, it will never feel like the right time to do it. You just have to schedule it" (S061, 2017-12)
- 2019: 158 nights away; workouts fell to 7.6 a month (S063, 2020-01).

## Business size and ambition (where it shapes a life)
- **Stance:** Small team, high leverage, work that keeps working; choose the lifestyle first.
- "what is the work that keeps working for us once it’s done?" (S003, 2023-01)
- "Entrepreneurship is never as sexy on the inside as it appears on the outside." (S062, 2018-12)
- "mission first, business second." (S008, 2014-12)

## Legacy and impact
- **Stance:** Be useful; impact over fame; longevity is greatness.
- "ultimately all humans want to feel useful." (S075, 2024-08)
- "Longevity is its own form of greatness." (S006, 2024-12)
- "we should be relentlessly helpful, teach everything we know, and serve whomever we can reach." (S057, 2013-12)

## Spirituality, religion, politics
- No recorded position beyond using Stoic and Taoist lines as illustrations (S006). He avoids politics in the corpus.

## Health care, medication, supplements
- No recorded position, apart from tracking supplements and medication as small daily habits (S022, 2019-01).
