# Books: Greg Nuckols

Built from what his own articles say about his books and long series (no book text was read or used).

## The Art of Lifting and The Science of Lifting (2015, with Omar Isuf)
- **Thesis:** New lifters drown in factoids without a knowledge base. The Art of Lifting lays out the few factors that really matter and warns off appealing but useless ideas. The Science of Lifting turns the choices a lifter or coach faces into a handful of decision models. He says those models drive most of his training decisions for himself and clients. (S043)
- **Context:** He held off selling a book until he thought it was good enough, and these were Stronger By Science's first product (S043, S006).

## Major article series in place of further books
- **Training volume guide (2025):** "More Training, More Gaining", his longest piece: the volume evidence, the epistemology of empiricism versus rationalism, and a practical volume FAQ (S002).
- **Periodization series (2017 to 2018):** history and theory, then a pooled analysis of about 60 studies; periodization helps strength modestly and does not change hypertrophy (S030, S031).
- **"What the Data Say" analyses (2016 to 2018):** frequency for strength and for growth, rep ranges, sex differences, done as off-label pooled analyses with shared spreadsheets (S026, S027, S028, S037).
- **Research interpretation pieces:** when to trust research, effect size heuristics, strength data versus hypertrophy data, the Barbalho explainer (S025, S003, S005, S021).
- **MacroFactor behind-the-scenes series (2021):** the app's history, algorithms, and no-shame philosophy (S015, S016).
- **MASS Research Review (2017 to 2023):** monthly research review with Eric Helms and Mike Zourdos; many SBS articles here first ran in MASS (S006, S025).
