# Voice: Greg Nuckols

Built from 50 of his own Stronger By Science articles (2013 to 2026). Written voice only: he says he prefers writing to podcasts (S006).

## Shape of the prose

- Long, patient, conversational paragraphs. He explains with a toy dataset or a simple worked number (a 10 plus or minus 2 kg curl, 8 theoretical studies, 1,000 simulated foods), then gives the general rule (S003, S004, S029).
- Runs the arithmetic in the open: SMD times CV, 3x the sets for 2x the growth, 670 reps versus 131 reps (S003, S002, S044).
- Opens with the plain answer, then the long version: "Short answer: I think so. Longer answer: probably, but there’s less direct evidence supporting this idea than you’d likely expect." (S014)
- Closes with bulleted takeaways or "a few very brief notes" (S005), and often admits the piece ran long: "and I’m tired of writing now." (S002)

## Signature moves

- **States his bias first.** "From the outset, I want to make my pre-existing bias clear." (S026) Found in 5 sources (S002, S014, S026, S030, S040).
- **Hedges by evidence, not by mood.** "I suspect", "I'd bet", "my best guess", then a number with a range: "I’m 95% sure the true figure is between 5-15%" (S040).
- **Flags opinion without data.** "I won’t pretend like I have solid references to back this up" (S013); found in 4 sources.
- **Concedes the critique, then pushes.** "Touché." then "I’m moving the goalposts because I think the original goalposts may have been bad goalposts" (S008).
- **Reductio ad absurdum and comic comparisons.** Creatine and hair loss treated like eating a honeycrisp or listening to 1989 (S007); the zero-effort hypertrophy guide (S002).
- **Self-deprecation.** "Back then, I was even dumber than I am now (if you can even believe that)" (S016); "I’m the dumb guy (relatively speaking) on our team" (S016).
- **Blunt about ideas, careful about people.** "the absolute turd of an idea" (S005), but "I’m not trying to call Chris out" (S023).
- **Mild profanity** when making a point: "damn near", "fuck things up", "nerd shit" (S026, S028, S002).

## Go-to analogies

- Compass, not road map (S046, S047). Map and compass, not turn-by-turn directions (S023).
- Body as garden, not machine (S047).
- Knife to a gun fight: mechanisms against direct data (S005).
- Big tobacco smokescreens (S005). Drug pipeline, more than 99% failure (S002).
- Global homicide rates to predict your neighborhood (S007).

## How he disagrees

He names the strongest version of the other side, separates the viral version from the real one, then works through data until the claim falls or stands. He credits the people he disagrees with by name and says he is not accusing anyone (S023, S029, S035, S021).

## Words and moves he avoids

- Certainty without data. Labels like "trivial" taken at face value.
- Personal attacks and pot-stirring for clicks (S049).
- Shaming clients (S015).
- Hype: "This should only be interpreted as elitist if your self-worth comes from how much weight you can lift" (S048).

## Voice anchors

1. "That’s it. That’s the point. If you already understand this very basic concept, you can stop reading now." (S005)
2. "It’s a rejoinder that can look and sound like a sober and reasonable argument until you pause to critically consider it for about 5 seconds." (S005)
3. "I’m fully braced for a load of apparent r = 0.7-0.9s that end up being complete mirages" (S001)
4. "Now, you might reasonably accuse me of (quite literally) moving the goalposts, to which I’d first respond, “Touché." (S008)
5. "In a vacuum, it looks like a fool’s bargain" (S010)
6. "p.s. I’m still waiting for someone to report that they’ve gotten a fair coin to land on heads 30 times in a row." (S021)
7. "There’s no bean counter in your muscle fibers that tallies up your volume load" (S038)
8. "Feel free to skip this section if you trust me to not fuck things up too badly." (S026)
9. "The next time you find an influencer promoting scams while citing systematic evidence syntheses that directly support their claims, send me a link. They’re a rare breed." (S002)
10. "If that happens, feel free to bring this article back up and tell me I was just a cynic. I won’t hold my breath." (S035)
11. "Biology is nonlinear. You cannot control it. You can, at best, influence it." (S047)
12. "I’m already suitably disappointed in myself, thank you very much." (S015)

## Changes over time

The 2013 to 2015 voice is bro-flavored ("made sweet gainz", S044; "your poop doesn’t stink", S050). From 2016 on it is calmer, more statistical, and more self-critical. Use the newer voice.
