# Books: Mike Israetel

Built from what the 35 sources say about his books. No book text was used. He co-wrote most of these RP books with colleagues (James Hoffmann, Melissa Davis, Jared Feather and others); the corpus does not give full author lists or exact years, so years below are approximate and marked.

## Scientific Principles of Hypertrophy Training (RP ebook, about 2021)
- **Core thesis:** Muscle growth follows a few principles (overload, fatigue management, specificity of stimulus, variation, individual difference) that can be programmed as numbers: volume landmarks, RIR, mesocycles and deloads.
- **Key frameworks (in my words):** volume landmarks, SFR, MEV-to-MRV mesocycles, set progression algorithm, exercise deletion and replacement, injury workarounds.
- **Taught in:** S033 (named as the RP training reference), S026 and S031 (the guide content), S020.

## How Much Should I Train? (RP ebook, before 2020)
- **Core thesis:** The book-length case for volume landmarks and within-meso volume progression.
- **Taught in:** S020 (cited as the detailed source), S026 ("our book on the subject of training volume").

## The Renaissance Diet 2.0 (RP book, about 2020) and the earlier RP Diet Book
- **Core thesis:** Diet results come in a fixed order of importance: calories, macros, timing, food composition, supplements. Diets run in phases (mass, maintain, cut) at controlled rates.
- **Key frameworks:** the priorities pyramid, phase structure, carbs biased around training, protein near 1 g per lb for lifters.
- **Taught in:** S015 and S033 (named), S022 (timing "as detailed and explained in the RP Diet Book"), S024, S029.

## RP Diet Templates and RP apps (products, not books)
- The diet templates (S029), RP Diet App (S021, S024, S028), and RP Hypertrophy App (S001, S026) put the same frameworks into software. He says he uses the hypertrophy app himself (S001).
