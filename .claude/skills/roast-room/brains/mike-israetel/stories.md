# Stories: Mike Israetel

Built from 35 sources (RP articles 2015 to 2024, podcast transcripts 2022 to 2025). 14 stories he tells, with the lesson he draws.

1. **The rushed photoshoot cut (2016).** Ten weeks from about 236 to 222 lbs, then the date moved up; he dropped to 150 g carbs and 45 g fat with 600 kcal cardio and got his best look, but calls it unsustainable. Lesson: start early, layer slowly, do not crash. (S022)
2. **BJJ six days a week while cutting.** Fatigue soared, gym training suffered, nagging injuries piled up. Now 3 BJJ sessions plus incline walking. Lesson: low intensity cardio does not steal recovery. (S022)
3. **Two shows not in shape.** He competed twice early without getting stage lean, and planned the next prep to be slower. Lesson: every cut he rushes less goes better. (S022)
4. **The slow post-cut mass (2016).** From about 230 toward 243 lbs: fixed calories, high protein, lots of fiber, no cheat meals for 3 weeks, steps kept up. Lesson: avoid both staying shredded and eating everything. (S030)
5. **405 for 5 sets of 10.** Rested 7 to 10 minutes between sets and threw up after the last three. Lesson: rest is set by what is limiting, not by a clock. (S001)
6. **The Bosu ball squat study.** People could squat only about 60% of their max on a Bosu ball. Lesson: instability kills force and growth. (S001)
7. **"I can't gain weight."** A man online was gaining half a pound a week and did not know it. Lesson: first check the claim is true. (S001)
8. **10 reps in reserve your whole life.** A lifter stuck at 100 lbs for 18 adds weight weekly and finds he could do 135. Lesson: programmed progression exposes sandbagging. (S001)
9. **Two jobs and poor recovery (2024).** Working long days on camera at RP, he was "on" all day and recovered poorly. Lesson: psychological load is fatigue too. (S003)
10. **Mike Stone's chairs.** His PhD mentor made athletes sit whenever a chair was near. Lesson: daily activity eats recovery. (S003)
11. **The lazy high jumper.** Dare him and he hits a PR, so he was recovered. Lesson: performance beats self-report. (S003)
12. **10,000 RP Diet App diets (2020).** Shorter diets, moderate rates, and following the app's adjustments did best; he checked for survivor bias. Lesson: moderate and short beats heroic. (S021)
13. **Dexter Jackson and Ronnie Coleman.** Jackson barely did cardio because of genetics; Coleman might have won 9 Olympias training better. Lesson: winners succeed partly in spite of their methods. (S034)
14. **First gym at 15.** A scary place where he was bad; 25 years later it is his spirit home. Lesson: make the gym rewarding and habit follows. (S001)
