# Books: Alan Aragon

Built from what he says about his books in the corpus (S020, S029, S034, S035, S037). No book text was read or reproduced.

## Girth Control: The Science of Fat Loss & Muscle Gain (2007)
- **Thesis:** His first book, self-published at Lyle McDonald's urging. A science-based guide to body composition built on calorie balance, macro targets, and realistic rates of change. (S020)
- **Note:** Lyle caught a factual error in it; correcting it pushed him to start his monthly research review in 2008 to stay buried in the literature. (S020) He later called it "an aging book" that still had value (S009 comment, paraphrase).

## Flexible Dieting: A Science-Based, Reality-Tested Method for Achieving and Maintaining Your Optimal Physique, Performance, and Health (Victory Belt, 2022)
- **Thesis:** Rigid, all-or-nothing dieting fails most people; a flexible approach with protein and calories fixed and carbs and fat set by preference works better and lasts. Written 2020 to 2022 from 30 years of field experience; some professors use it as a text. (S029, S034)
- **Key frameworks, in my words:** rigid versus flexible restraint; individualized degree of tracking; protein on target bodyweight (0.7 to 1.0 g/lb); deficit of 10 to 20 percent; fat floor about 20 percent or 0.3 g/lb; carbs by preference with a higher floor for muscle gain; a 10 to 20 percent discretionary margin; diet breaks every 4 to 8 weeks or 5 to 10 lb; two chapters on scientific literacy. (S034, S035, S036)
- **Taught in:** S034, S035, S036, S029, S037.

## Alan Aragon's Research Review (AARR), monthly since January 2008
- Not a book, but his main body of work: a monthly critique of nutrition, training, and supplement research, no sponsors, cheap on purpose. He calls it the first of its kind and his "baby". (S023, S031, S032, S038)
